## What you are doing

This folder is a sample book: §10600–§10700 of परमात्मप्रकाश (दोहा 1.60–1.70, with प्रक्षेपक 1.65*1). Each §
already has its full व्याख्या (`parts/` + `addenda/s<key>.md`), audited and collated against the print. **You add two
new parts to each `addenda/s<key>.md`, and change nothing else.** They follow the pattern the reader liked in our
मूलाचार व्याख्या (`E:\Projects\JinAagam\granth\mulachar\pass2_instructions.md`, sections 7-उद्देश्य and 9-आचरण —
read them), adapted to an **अध्यात्म** text.

The difference matters. मूलाचार prescribes conduct (क्रिया, समिति, व्रत). परमात्मप्रकाश prescribes **seeing**: a भावना,
a भेद-विज्ञान, a हेय-उपादेय judgement, a ध्यान. So the "practice" here is mostly inner — what to contemplate, how to
catch the opposite habit, how to test whether the seeing has taken root — and it is for any मुमुक्षु, गृहस्थ
श्रावक as well as साधु.

Read the whole § first: `parts/batch01_doha60-70.md` (मूल, छाया, printed अर्थ-भावार्थ, टीका) and the addenda (our
टीका-हिन्दी and parts 7–10). The टीका usually ends with a sentence like `अत्र … उपादेय इति भावार्थः` — that is
श्री ब्रह्मदेव's own statement of what the दोहा is for; build on it.

## Part 7-क — insert after `## 7.` and before `## 8.`

Heading exactly: `## 7-क. साधना का युक्तिसंगत उद्देश्य (प्रयोजन एवं कषाय-निवृत्ति)`

**Every भावना, साधना, हेय-उपादेय विवेक or ध्यान the दोहा (or its टीका) asks for gets one bullet:**

`- **<भावना/साधना>** — *उद्देश्य:* … । *युक्ति:* (why it achieves this — reasoning a thoughtful साधक would accept) … । *निवृत्ति:* the कषाय / मिथ्यात्व / दोष / विकल्प it cuts … ।`

- Take the उद्देश्य from the टीका where it states one (`… इति भावार्थः`, `… उपादेयम्`, `… निमित्तम्`) and say
  `टीका के अनुसार`. Where it is your own reasoning from the doctrine, say `व्याख्याकार का तर्क`. **Never present your
  own युक्ति as आगम-वचन.**
- A युक्ति must be an argument with a mechanism, not a restatement. Weak: "भेद-भावना से राग छूटता है।" Good: why
  seeing कर्म-फल as पर-कृत removes the ground on which अहंकार-ममकार stand, and which कषाय that starves.
- Name the specific कषाय / मिथ्यात्व / विकल्प only where the doctrine supports it; if none is the natural target,
  say what is.
- 2–4 bullets; 50–120 words each.

## Part 9-क — insert after `## 9.` and before `## 10.`

Heading exactly: `## 9-क. आचरण-विधि एवं अभ्यास (जीवन में उतारने के सूत्र)`

**Write it for a मुमुक्षु who wants to *live* this दोहा, not only understand it.** 3–6 bullets, 40–100 words each,
each starting with its tag in bold:

- **चिन्तन.** — what to reflect on, when (प्रातः / सामायिक-काल / स्वाध्याय के बाद / रात्रि में सोने से पहले / किसी
  दुःख या प्रशंसा के क्षण में), and the question to ask oneself.
- **अभ्यास.** — a concrete drill: the steps, and **how often** (`दिन में तीन बार`, `प्रत्येक क्रोध के क्षण में एक बार`,
  `एक सप्ताह तक केवल यही भावना`) so it becomes natural.
- **चर्चा.** — a question to take to गुरु / स्वाध्याय-मण्डली, and what a good answer would contain.
- **परीक्षण.** — how to tell it is taking root (a sign), the lapse to watch for, and the common mistake that spoils
  it (e.g. turning निश्चय-दृष्टि into an excuse to drop व्यवहार-धर्म — guard against this explicitly where the दोहा
  speaks in शुद्ध-निश्चय terms).
- **परिस्थिति.** — one hard case (illness, insult, loss, success) where this is tested.
- Optionally one line **श्रावक के लिए.** / **साधु के लिए.** where the practice differs.

Rules:
1. **Source honesty.** `[आगम-सम्मत]` after anything the टीका or the आगम states; `[सुझाव]` after the व्याख्याकार's own
   suggestion. **No suggested frequency may be passed off as आगम-विधान.** If the आगम gives no number, say so and offer
   one as `[सुझाव]`.
2. **Nothing against the आगम.** A technique must serve the very भावना the दोहा teaches — no new vow, no new rule, no
   softening of a rule, and no निश्चयाभास: never suggest that व्यवहार-चारित्र (पूजा, दान, व्रत, सामायिक) can be
   dropped because "the soul is pure".
3. **Do not invent an authority.** A drill is a drill, not "शास्त्र कहता है" unless it does.
4. If the दोहा allows no separate practice, still give the one practice it makes possible.
