# Pass-1 flags — readings the extraction agents could not settle

*Working document. Nothing here goes into the book.*

Every extraction agent reports doubtful readings rather than quietly emending them. They are collected here
so the audit pass (pass 3) has them in one place, and so none is lost between batches.

**How to use this file.** When auditing a § listed below, settle its flag as well as checking the Hindi.
The order of attack that has worked:

1. **Read the edition's own हिन्दी** for that दोहा — it glosses the टीका's terms and settles most doubts
   outright, with no magnification at all.
2. **Rejoin any word split across a line end** before reading it.
3. **If it is a quoted verse, recall the received text** of the ग्रन्थ it comes from.
4. **Test the word lexically** — a sequence that is not a Sanskrit word is a misreading, not the edition.
5. Only then magnify.

Record the outcome against each entry: `RESOLVED → <reading>` with the evidence, or `STANDS AS PRINTED`.

### §20430 (T8) — `उपशमाहारविस्तारधर्मा` in a quoted श्लोक
A three-way tension, and the most interesting flag so far:

- the **print** reads `…स्वदेहप्रमितिरुपशमाहारविस्तारधर्मा…` clearly, even at 1200 dpi;
- the **हिन्दी** glosses it "प्रदेशोंके **संकोच विस्तार**को धारण करता है" (contraction–expansion);
- the **टीका's own prose**, two lines earlier, uses `उपसंहारविस्तार` twice for the same idea.

Two witnesses agree against the print, so the quoted श्लोक most likely wants `उपसंहारविस्तार`. **And the
edition itself flags this very श्लोक**: the footnote on मुद्रित पृष्ठ 164 reads
*"यह श्लोक अपूर्ण है, भाषामें 'नाभाव' आदि लिखा है।"* — the editor knew the quotation was defective.
Render the sense the हिन्दी and the prose give, keep the print in part 10 with the editor's own footnote,
and say plainly that the quotation is corrupt in the source.

## SEAM CHECK — RESOLVED, no action needed

**A महाधिकार-closing colophon sits exactly on the B25/B26 boundary (scan p0386/p0387).** B26 saw it at the
top of its first page, correctly judged it belonged to the previous batch, and did not transcribe it. B25
had not yet reported when this was noticed, so **nobody has confirmed B25 captured it.** This is the दोहा-12
failure mode applied to a colophon, and it would be invisible to `check_coverage.py`.

The text to look for, at the tail of दोहा 107's टीका:

> `… इति षोडशवर्णिकासुवर्णदृष्टान्तेन … त्रयोदशसूत्रैरन्तरस्थलं गतम् । एवं मोक्षमोक्षफल…द्वितीयमहाधिकारमध्ये
> चतुर्भिरन्तरस्थलैः … एकचत्वारिंशत्सूत्रैर्महास्थलं समाप्तम् ।`

**RESOLVED.** B25 did capture it, as `(T12)`–`(T13)` of §21070, having read two pages past its nominal
range to close both streams and both colophons. It also caught the प्र/म inside it — the file has
`सर्वजीवसमानता**प्रतिपादन**मुख्यत्वेनैकचत्वारिंशत्सूत्रैः`, zoom-confirmed at 900 dpi.

`check_colophons.py` now finds it and reads its count, and the arithmetic closes: the महास्थल's four
अन्तरस्थल are **5 + 15 + 8 + 13 = 41**, exactly the महास्थल's own stated total, each number coming from a
different batch written by a different agent.

### Cross-batch sweep: `देहममल` → `देहममत्वं` — RESOLVED in two more places
Three agents met this word in three different batches. One corrected it (`देहममत्वं त्यक्त्वा`), two left
`देहममलं` standing. `ममल` is not a word; `ममत्व` occurs 14× clean here and the idiom `देहममत्वं त्यक्त्वा`
appears verbatim in the corrected batch. Fixed in §20430 (`देहममलं` → `देहममत्वं`) and §21070
(`देहममलमूलभूतानां` → `देहममत्वमूलभूतानां`).

**Beware the near-miss:** `कर्ममल` (कर्म + मल, "the stain of karma") is genuine and occurs 8× — the `ममल`
sequence there is innocent. Only `देह + ममल` is impossible.

This is the clearest case so far that **a per-batch review cannot find what a corpus sweep finds.** Each
agent's decision was locally defensible; only reading all 35 files together showed two of the three were
wrong.
