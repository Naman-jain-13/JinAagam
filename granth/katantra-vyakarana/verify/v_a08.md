## A. पाठ-सुधार (parts/)
The main finding: in this fount the conjunct **श्र** has a श-like loop, while **अ** looks like ग्र. Both the bold sūtra heading and the vṛtti, on p39 and p40, show श्र. Every `अद्धा` in this range is printed **श्रद्धा**, the Kātantra name for a feminine ā-stem. The headline of 2.1.10 on p33 is `आ श्रद्धा ॥१०॥`, and batch05 (§40010) already writes श्रद्धा.
