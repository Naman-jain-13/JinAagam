# Boundaries adhikar 6 / 7 in Mulachar_Purvardh_Aryika_Jnanmati.pdf (printed = scan - 58, verified)

1. Adhikar 6 (pindashuddhi) ends on scan p442-1 = **scan 441** (printed 383): closing "इत्याचारवृत्तौ वसुनन्दिविरचितायां पिण्डशुद्धिनाम षष्ठः प्रस्तावः", last gatha **501** (jenna pindasuddhi ... visantu ||501||; footnote adds an extra mangala gatha, unnumbered).
2. Adhikar 7 (shadavashyaka) title page = **scan 442** (printed 384), "७. षडावश्यकाधिकारः"; first gatha **502** (kauna namokkaram arahantanam ...).
3. Adhikar 7 does NOT end at 682. Gathas 683-684 are on scan 551, and the numbering runs on to **692**.
   - Last gatha **692** (avasayaniujjutti evam kahida ... ||692||) is on **scan 558** (printed 500). Fount draws 9 like 6: sequence checked 688 (scan 555) -> 690, 691 (scan 557) -> 692 (scan 558).
   - Closing line "इति श्रीवट्टकेराचार्यवर्यप्रणीतमूलाचारस्य वसुनन्दाचार्यविरचितायामाचारवृत्तावावश्यकनिर्युक्तिनामकः सप्तमः परिच्छेदः ॥७॥" is on **scan 562** (printed 504), top of page, after Hindi visheshartha / vidhi text on scans 559-561 (no Sanskrit gathas there; 559-561 are Hindi + Prakrit krtikarma prayer text).
   - So the Uttarardh volume starting at 683 means a gatha numbering mismatch/overlap: this volume contains all of adhikar 7 (502-692); verify what the Uttarardh actually starts with.
4. Back matter (not text): scan **563-573** gathanukramanika (gatha index; 563 title), **574-578** paribhashik shabda kosh, **579** inside back cover (Aryika Jnanmati note), **580** back cover.
