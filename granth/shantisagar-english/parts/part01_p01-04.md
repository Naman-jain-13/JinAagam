# Life-Sketch of Charitra Chakravarti Acharya 108 Shri Shantisagar Ji Maharaj (Dakshin)

*The First Acharya of the 20th Century, Supremely Venerable, Ever to Be Remembered at Dawn*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Birth** | Ashadha Krishna 6, V.S. 1929 (1872 CE), a Wednesday. |
| **Birthplace and name** | Yelgul (Maharashtra); Satgauda Patil (Jain). |
| **Parents** | Shrimati Satyavati and Bhimgauda Patil. |
| **Kshullaka Diksha** | Jyeshtha Shukla 13, V.S. 1972 (1915 CE), at Uttur village (Maharashtra). |
| **Ailaka Diksha** | At Girnar Ji Siddhakshetra, at the feet of Bhagwan Neminath on the fifth *tonk* (peak). |
| **Muni Diksha** | Phalguna Shukla 14, V.S. 1976 (1920 CE), at Yarnal village (Karnataka). |
| **Diksha-Guru for Kshullaka and Muni Diksha** | Muni Shri Devendrakirti Ji Maharaj. |
| **Acharya Pada (Acharya-ship)** | Ashwina Shukla 11, V.S. 1981 (1924 CE), at Samdoli village, on a Wednesday. On that very day Acharya Shri conferred 2 Muni dikshas, 1 Ailaka diksha and 1 Kshullika diksha. |
| **Charitra Chakravarti (title)** | V.S. 1994 (1937 CE), on the auspicious occasion of the Panchakalyanak Pratishtha at Gajpantha Siddhakshetra. |
| **Auspicious vihar and chaturmas in North India** | Marga­shirsha Krishna 1, V.S. 1984 (1927 CE), setting out to pay homage at Tirthraj Sammed Shikhar Ji, at the request of Seth Gendamal Ji of Bombay, the *sanghapati*. Completed 11 chaturmas in North India from 1928 onward. |
| **Pada-vihar (journeys on foot)** | Travelled more than 35,000 miles on foot during his life of restraint. |
| **Tapa-sadhana (austerities)** | While performing many vows such as Simhanishkridita, Charitrashuddhi, Tees Chaubisi and Kavalachandrayana, he completed a total of 9,938 fasts (*upavasa*) in his lifetime, along with *rasa-parityaga* (renunciation of tastes) and many other external and internal austerities. |
| **Total chaturmas** | Completed 41 chaturmas during his initiated life. |
| **Initiated disciples** | 26 Munis, 4 Aryikas, 16 Ailakas, 28 Kshullakas, 14 Kshullikas, and a fourfold sangha of hundreds of vow-taking (*vrati*) shravakas. |
| **Special** | In 1925 he graced with his presence the *Mahamastakabhisheka* of Gommateshwar Bhagwan Bahubali Swami. From Shravanabelagola onward he came to be called *"Gurunam Guru"* — the Guru of Gurus. |
| | For the protection of Jain temples he gave up taking grain (*anna*) for 3 years and 12 days, thereby protecting the Dharma and its culture. |
| | To protect the *Shatkhandagama* and *Mahabandha* scriptures — written on palm-leaves — from insects, he had those texts engraved on copper plates. |
| **Adoption of Utkrishta Sallekhana** | On 24 October 1951 (V.S. 2008) he adopted the twelve-year Utkrishta (supreme) Sallekhana at Gajpantha Siddhakshetra. |
| **Conferring his own Acharya Pada** | In the midst of his 36-day Sallekhana, on 26 August 1955, he had a letter written and conferred the Acharya Pada upon his first *nirgrantha* (possessionless) disciple, Muni Shri Veersagar Ji. |
| **Last discourse** | On 8 September 1955, the 26th day of the 36-day Sallekhana, he gave his last discourse, in the Marathi language, for 22 minutes. |
| **Passing away with Sallekhana** | On Bhadrapada Shukla 2, 18 September 1955, at 6:50 in the morning, at Kunthalgiri Siddhakshetra in the province of Maharashtra, he left his body with Sallekhana. |
