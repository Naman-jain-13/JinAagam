# Pooja of Charitra Chakravarti Acharya 108 Shri Shantisagar Ji Mahamuniraj

*Translator's note on this section: The Pooja is a devotional text meant for recitation. Each verse is therefore given first in Roman transliteration (so that it can be recited as in the original) and then in English meaning. The Sanskrit mantras that accompany each offering are given in transliteration, with the meaning of the recurring formula explained once.*

## Sthapana (Invocation) — to the tune of "Prabhu Patit Pavan"

*He Shantisagar shantikarta, shanti ke datar ho.*
*Kalikal pancham manhi aakar, dharma ke kartar ho.*
*Charon gati mein bhraman kina, dukh sahe bharpur jo.*
*Ham sharan aaye nath teri, kasht sare door ho.*

O Shantisagar, bringer of peace, giver of peace! Having come into this fifth age of Kali, you are the doer of Dharma. We who have wandered through the four states of existence and suffered pain in full — we have come to your refuge, O Lord; may all our troubles be removed.

**Doha (couplet):**
*Aahvanan ham kar rahe, hriday virajo aay.*
*Paap karam sab nasht hon, bhav bhav hoy sahay.*

We are making the invocation; come and be seated in our heart. May all sinful karmas be destroyed; be our helper in birth after birth.

*Om Hreem Charitra Chakravarti Acharya 108 Shri Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo atra avatara avatara samvaushat — aahvananam.* (Invocation: "O Charitra Chakravarti Acharya 108 Shri Shantisagar, foremost of great ascetics, descend here, descend here.")
*Om Hreem Charitra Chakravarti Acharya 108 Shri Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo atra tishtha tishtha thah thah — sthapanam.* (Establishment: "…abide here, abide here.")
*Om Hreem Charitra Chakravarti Acharya 108 Shri Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo atra mama sannihito bhava bhava vashat — sannidhikaranam.* (Drawing near: "…be present close to me, be present.")

## Ashtaka (The Eight Offerings) — Narendra metre

**Jala (Water)**
*Janam maran ko karte karte, kaal anant gamaye hain.*
*Aavagaman mita do Guruvar, jal bhar kalasha laye hain.*
*Shantisagar Ji mahatapasvi, mangalkari sukhadai.*
*Kalikal ka dosh mitakar, municharya ko batlai.*

Being born and dying, again and again, we have let endless time pass. Remove our coming and going (transmigration), O great Guru — we have brought a *kalasha* filled with water. Shantisagar Ji, the great ascetic, the auspicious one, the giver of happiness, wiped away the blemish of the Kali age and showed the conduct of a Muni.

*Om Hreem Charitra Chakravarti Acharya 108 Shri Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo janma-jara-mrityu-vinashanaya jalam nirvapamiti svaha.* ("…for the destruction of birth, old age and death, I offer water.")

**Chandana (Sandal-paste)**
*Bhavavan ki is maha anal mein, ab tak jalta aaya hoon.*
*Bhav ka taap mitado Guruvar, chandan charan chadhaya hoon. — Shantisagar.*

In this great fire of the forest of worldly existence I have been burning until now. Remove the heat of *samsara*, O great Guru — I have offered sandal at your feet. — Shantisagar.

*Om Hreem … Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo samsara-taapa-vinashanaya chandanam nirvapamiti svaha.* ("…for the destruction of the heat of *samsara*, I offer sandal.")

**Akshata (Unbroken rice)**
*Koi bhi thir na is jag mein, kshan mein nashvar ho jaye.*
*Akshaya pad pane ke karan, akshat lekar ham aaye. — Shantisagar.*

Nothing in this world is stable; in a moment it becomes perishable. To attain the imperishable state, we have come bringing unbroken rice. — Shantisagar.

*Om Hreem … Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo akshaya-pada-praptaye akshatan nirvapamiti svaha.* ("…for the attainment of the imperishable state, I offer unbroken rice.")

**Pushpa (Flowers)**
*Maha tapasvi tap ke bal se, Kamadev ka mad chhina.*
*Kamabaan ke naash karan ko, pushpa charan mein dhar dina. — Shantisagar.*

The great ascetic, by the power of his austerity, stripped Kamadeva (the god of desire) of his pride. To destroy the arrows of desire, I have placed flowers at your feet. — Shantisagar.

*Om Hreem … Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo kamabana-vidhvamsanaya pushpam nirvapamiti svaha.* ("…for the destruction of the arrows of desire, I offer flowers.")

**Naivedya (Food-offering)**
*Kshudha rog se peedit hokar, ab tak bahu dukh paye hain.*
*Kshudha dakini door bhagado, shatras vyanjan laye hain. — Shantisagar.*

Afflicted by the disease of hunger, we have suffered much until now. Drive away the demoness of hunger — we have brought dishes of the six flavours. — Shantisagar.

*Om Hreem … Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo kshudha-roga-vinashanaya naivedyam nirvapamiti svaha.* ("…for the destruction of the disease of hunger, I offer food.")

**Deepa (Lamp)**
*Mithyatam se raah na dikhe, tav charanon mein aaye hain.*
*Gyan ujala karo hamara, deep jalakar laye hain. — Shantisagar.*

In the darkness of *mithyatva* (false belief) we cannot see the path; we have come to your feet. Kindle the light of knowledge in us — we have brought a lit lamp. — Shantisagar.

*Om Hreem … Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo mohandhakara-vinashanaya deepam nirvapamiti svaha.* ("…for the destruction of the darkness of delusion, I offer a lamp.")

**Dhoopa (Incense)**
*Dasavidh dhoop anal mein khevoon, maha sugandhit laoonga.*
*Asht karam ki dhoom udakar, Shivramani pa jaoonga. — Shantisagar.*

I offer ten-fold incense into the fire and bring great fragrance. Sending up the smoke of the eight karmas, I shall attain the Bride of Liberation (*Shiva-ramani*). — Shantisagar.

*Om Hreem … Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo ashtakarma-dahanaya dhoopam nirvapamiti svaha.* ("…for the burning of the eight karmas, I offer incense.")

**Phala (Fruit)**
*Nishphal ab tak bhatak raha hoon, raah kahin na payi hai.*
*Mahamoksha phal prapt karan ko, teri pooj rachai hai. — Shantisagar.*

Fruitlessly I have wandered until now; nowhere have I found the path. To obtain the great fruit of *moksha*, I have composed this worship of you. — Shantisagar.

*Om Hreem … Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo moksha-phala-praptaye phalam nirvapamiti svaha.* ("…for the attainment of the fruit of liberation, I offer fruit.")

**Arghya (The combined offering)**
*Jal chandan akshat charu deepak, dhoop phool phal le aaye.*
*Asht dravya se pooj rachakar, Siddha shila ko pa javen.*
*Shantisagar Ji mahatapasvi, mangalkari sukhadai.*
*Kalikal ka dosh mitakar, municharya ko batlai.*

Water, sandal, unbroken rice, food, lamp, incense, flowers and fruit — we have brought them. Composing this worship with the eight substances, may we attain the *Siddha Shila* (the abode of the liberated). Shantisagar Ji, the great ascetic, the auspicious one, the giver of happiness, wiped away the blemish of the Kali age and showed the conduct of a Muni.

*Om Hreem … Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo anargha-pada-praptaye arghyam nirvapamiti svaha.* ("…for the attainment of the priceless state, I offer *arghya*.")

**Shanti-dhara (Stream of Peace)**
*Shanti dhara ham karen, shanti pane het.*
*Shanti Guru ke charan mein, pooja bhav samet.* — *Shantaye Shantidhara.*

We pour the stream of peace for the attainment of peace, at the feet of the Guru of Peace, together with the feeling of worship. — The stream of peace, for peace.

**Pushpanjali (Handful of flowers)**
*Nana vidhi ke suman le, deoon charan chadhay.*
*Prabhav bhakti teri kare, kamabaan nash jaay.* — *Divya Pushpanjali.*

Taking flowers of many kinds, I offer them at your feet. May Prabhav (the composer) perform your devotion; may the arrows of desire be destroyed. — The divine handful of flowers.

**Mantra:** *Om Hreem Charitra Chakravarti Acharya 108 Shri Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo namah* (9 times).

## Jayamala (Garland of Victory) — Chaupai metre

*Shantiguru tum ho hitkari, sab jeevon par karuna dhari.*
*Charon gati mein ghoom liya hai, tumne jag ka chhor liya hai.*
Guru of Peace, you are the benefactor, bearing compassion for all living beings. You have wandered through the four states of existence; you have reached the far shore of the world.

*Satyavati mata ur aaye, Bhimgaud ke tanuj kahaye.*
*Jo bhi muni nagari mein aate, kandhon se nadi paar karate.*
You came into the womb of mother Satyavati and were called the son of Bhimgauda. Whatever Munis came to the town, you carried them across the river on your shoulders.

*Phagun sudi chaudas jab aayi, municharya ban kar dikhlayi.*
*Ashubh yog na man mein aave, shubh yogon se dhyan lagave.*
When Phalguna Shukla Chaturdashi came, you took up the Muni's conduct and showed it. No inauspicious activity entered your mind; you fixed your meditation through auspicious activities.

*Teen ashubh leshya ab nahin, shubh leshya hi shesh rahai.*
*Nana upasarg sahe bahu bhari, apne man mein samata dhari.*
The three inauspicious *leshyas* (colourings of the soul) were no more; only the auspicious *leshyas* remained. You bore many very heavy afflictions, holding equanimity in your mind.

*Nagraj tum par chadh aaya, Simharaj se bhay nahin khaya.*
*Achal dhyan ke ho tum swami, samata ke sagar tum nami.*
The king of serpents climbed upon you; you felt no fear of the king of lions. You are the master of unshakeable meditation; you are renowned as the ocean of equanimity.

*Man mein khed ka nahin thikana, Shanti Sagar daya nidhana.*
*Nikat bhavya tum nath rahavo, alp kaal mein Shivpur pavo.*
In your mind there was no trace of distress, O Shantisagar, treasure of compassion. O Lord, you are a *nikata-bhavya* (soul near to liberation); in a short time you will attain the City of Liberation.

*Jeevan sara tap mein beeta, karam bali ko tumne jeeta.*
*Panchamkaal mahadukhadai, samyam ki charya batlai.*
Your whole life passed in austerity; you conquered the mighty karma. In the fifth age, the giver of great sorrow, you showed the conduct of self-restraint.

*Uttar dish mein tum na aate, kahin ek na sadhu paate.*
*Agam ke anukool bataya, kahin na usmein avgun paya.*
Had you not come to the northern region, not a single *sadhu* would have been found anywhere. You taught in accordance with the Agam; no fault was ever found in it.

*Simha samaan maha baldhari, hathi sam the unnat bhari.*
*Pavan samaan vicharne wale, roke se na rukne wale.*
Mighty in strength like a lion, lofty and grand like an elephant; wandering like the wind, not to be stopped by any hindrance.

*Meru sam tha dhyan tumhara, karma-shatru bhi tumse hara.*
*Surya samaan tej ke dhari, chandra-kiran sam sheetal bhari.*
Your meditation was like Mount Meru; even the enemy karma was defeated by you. Bearer of brilliance like the sun, and cool like the rays of the moon.

*Ashwin shubh Ekadashi aaya, Acharaj pad tumne paya.*
*Charitra-chakri aap kahaye, shat-avashyak hamen bataye.*
The auspicious Ashwina Ekadashi came; you received the Acharya Pada. You were called the Charitra Chakravarti; you taught us the six essential duties (*shad-avashyaka*).

*Goonga bole, langda chale, kushti apna kusht mitale.*
*Dukhiya jo charanon mein aaya, sankat uska door bhagaya.*
The dumb spoke, the lame walked, the leper had his leprosy removed. Whoever came in distress to your feet — you drove his trouble far away.

*Indriya panchon vash mein keeni, shatkayon ki raksha keeni.*
*Jin mandir par sankat aaya, Guruvar tumne use bachaya.*
You brought the five senses under control; you protected the six classes of living beings. When a crisis came upon the Jain temples, O great Guru, you saved them.

*Jinavani ki raksha keeni, tamrapatra par likhva deeni.*
*Ham sab tav charanon mein aaye, bhav sagar se ham tir jayen.*
You protected the *Jinavani* (the word of the Jina); you had it written on copper plates. We have all come to your feet; may we cross the ocean of existence.

**Doha:**
*Teen lok kagaj karoon, lekhani sab banaray.*
*Sab sagar syahi karoon, Guru gun likha na jaay.*
Were I to make the three worlds my paper, all the forests my pens, and all the oceans my ink — still the virtues of the Guru could not be written.

*Om Hreem Shri Charitra Chakravarti Acharya 108 Shri Shantisagar Mahayativarebhyo jayamala poornargham nirvapamiti svaha.* ("…I offer the full *arghya* of the Jayamala.")

**Doha:**
*Guruvar tere naam se, bigde bante kaam.*
*Prabhav hit badhta rahe, Shivpur mein ho dhaam.*
O great Guru, by your name ruined works are set right. May the welfare of Prabhav (the composer) keep increasing; may his abode be in the City of Liberation.

*Ityashirvadah — divya pushpanjalim kshipet.* (Thus the benediction — cast the divine handful of flowers.)

## Arghya to Charitra Chudamani Acharya 108 Shri Veersagar Ji Maharaj

*Jal phal aathon dravya sajakar, swarna thaal bhar laye hain.*
*Bhav se paar laga do Guruvar, pooja paath rachaye hain.*
*Shanti Sindhu ne patta diya hai, pratham patta kahlate hain.*
*Path bhoole hain raah batado, charanan sheesh namate hain.*

Arranging the eight substances — water, fruit and the rest — we have brought them heaped in a golden plate. Carry us across worldly existence, O great Guru; we have composed this worship. The Ocean of Peace (Shantisagar) gave you the seat (*patta*); you are called the first *patta*-holder. We have lost the path — show us the way; we bow our heads at your feet.

*Om Hreem Charitra Chudamani Acharya Shri Veersagar Ji argham nirvapamiti svaha.*

## Arghya to Siddhanta Samrakshaka (Protector of the Doctrine) Acharya 108 Shri Shivsagar Ji Maharaj

*Panchachar ka palan karte, shishyon se karvate hain.*
*Nath aapke pad pankaj se, bhavsagar tir jate hain.*
*Shivsagar Guru Shiv ke rahi, Shiv ki raah bata dena.*
*Bhavsagar mein doob rahe, jag se paar laga dena.*

You observe the fivefold conduct (*panchachara*) and have your disciples observe it. O Lord, by your lotus feet we cross the ocean of existence. Guru Shivsagar, traveller on the road to Liberation — show us the road to Liberation. We are drowning in the ocean of existence; carry us across the world.

*Om Hreem Siddhanta Samrakshaka Acharya 108 Shri Shivsagar Ji argham nirvapamiti svaha.*

## Arghya to Dharma Shiromani Acharya 108 Shri Dharmasagar Ji Maharaj

*Par ko ab tak mana maine, bhool anadi chhaya hai.*
*Nij mein nij ka gun pane ko, pooja paath rachaya hai.*
*Dharmasindhu Guru Dharmaveer ho, Dharma dhwaja ke dhari ho.*
*Tum ho bhole Baba Guruvar, sat sat naman hamari ho.*

Until now I have taken the "other" (non-self) to be mine; a beginningless error has overshadowed me. To find the qualities of the self within the self, I have composed this worship. Guru, Ocean of Dharma, you are the hero of Dharma, the bearer of the banner of Dharma. You are the guileless "Baba," O great Guru; our salutations to you a hundred times over.

*Om Hreem Dharma Shiromani 108 Acharya Shri Dharmasagar Ji argham nirvapamiti svaha.*
