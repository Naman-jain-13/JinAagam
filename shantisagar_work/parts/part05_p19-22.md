**Question 107 —** Who gave hot milk into Maharaj's hands, and what happened to Acharya Shri as a result?
Answer — A shravaka gave very hot milk as food, from which Maharaj fainted and fell to the ground at once.

**Question 108 —** In which village did Maharaj have the throat ailment, and what else happened there?
Answer — In Kavlana there was pain in the throat. He had given up grain. He took food on alternate days. Because of continuous obstruction (*antaraya*) he once took food after six days. The body became emaciated. The severity of the cold increased. At night there was a violent fit of coughing; the breath that went out with it did not return for some time. The pulse also stopped, and the body became inert.

**Question 109 —** Where did a lion first come near Maharaj?
Answer — While he was meditating in the caves of Gokak village.

**Question 110 —** After Gokak, how many times did a lion come near Acharya Shri, and where?
Answer — At Muktagiri, Shravanabelagola, Dronagiri and Sonagiri; and several times in the lonely forest on the way from Muktagiri to Badwani.

**Question 111 —** Who conferred the Acharya Pada on Muni Shantisagar Maharaj, where and when?
Answer — The fourfold Sangha conferred it at Samdoli (Maharashtra), on Ashwina Shukla Ekadashi, a Wednesday, Samvat 1981, 1924 CE.

**Question 112 —** On the day Shantisagar Maharaj was given the Acharya Pada, to whom did he give *Muni diksha*?
Answer — He gave *Muni diksha* to Kshullaka Veersagar Ji and to Nemisagar Maharaj.

**Question 113 —** When was Veersagar Ji given *Kshullaka diksha*?
Answer — On Phalguna Shukla Saptami, V.S. 1980.

**Question 114 —** What was Nemisagar Ji's name before he took *Muni diksha*?
Answer — Shri Nemanna.

**Question 115 —** Where was Acharya Shantisagar Maharaj given the title *Charitra Chakravarti*?
Answer — At Gajpantha Siddhakshetra.

**Question 116 —** When Maharaj was having the idols of other deities loaded onto carts in Jainbadi and immersed in the river, the king asked the reason; what did Maharaj say?
Answer — "Just as, in your tradition, in the month of Bhadrapada, after the work is done, you immerse the Ganapati idols in the river — in the same way the period of worship of these has ended, so we too are immersing them in the river."

**Question 117 —** Why does the Jain viewpoint forbid *mithyatva*? What did Maharaj say on this subject?
Answer — "Innocent people, out of ignorance, give the names of gods and goddesses to countless false things and, worshipping them, destroy their priceless human birth."

**Question 118 —** When did Acharya Shantisagar Maharaj, with the Sangha, make the pilgrimage to Shravanabelagola?
Answer — In 1924.

**Question 119 —** In South India, the Sangha of Acharya Shri Shantisagar Maharaj accepted food only from those whom it had made give up what?
Answer — From those whom it had made give up *mithyatva*.

**Question 120 —** Why did Acharya Shri travel toward North India?
Answer — To put an end to the growing laxity in conduct (*shithilachara*) in the Jain community.

**Question 121 —** When did Acharya Shri's Sangha set out toward North India?
Answer — On Margashirsha Krishna Pratipada, saying "*Om Namah Siddhebhyah*."

**Question 122 —** Apart from Acharya Shri, how many Munis were with him? Name them.
Answer — There were three Munis: Muni Shri Nemisagar Ji, Veersagar Ji and Anantkirti Ji.

**Question 123 —** How many Ailakas were with Acharya Shri on the northward journey, and from where?
Answer — There were three Ailakas (all three named Payasagar), from Ainapur, Gokak and Shivapur.

**Question 124 —** How many Kshullakas were with Acharya Shri?
Answer — Three: Mallisagar, Payasagar and Anantkirti.

**Question 125 —** How many Kshullikas were in the Sangha at the time of the northward journey?
Answer — Three: Shantimati, Chandramati and Anantmati.

**Question 126 —** At the start of the northward journey, how many shravakas and shravikas were with Acharya Shri?
Answer — About 200 shravakas, shravikas and ascetics (*sadhu-sadhvi*).

**Question 127 —** Who was the *sanghapati* (leader/organiser) of Acharya Shri's Sangha?
Answer — Seth Poonam Chand Ghasilal of Bombay.

**Question 128 —** What did Acharya Shri do to end laxity of conduct in North India?
Answer — The Sangha adopted this rule: it would accept food only from those who had given up unfiltered water and would take *maryadit* (restricted, within-limit) food together with purity of food and drink.

**Question 129 —** From where did Acharya Shri's Sangha begin (*shubharambha*) its journey to the north (Shikhar Ji)?
Answer — From Kumbhoj Bahubali (Maharashtra).

**Question 130 —** On which day did Acharya Shri's Sangha reach Sammed Shikhar Ji?
Answer — On Phalguna Shukla Tritiya, 1927.

**Question 131 —** When was the Panchakalyanak held at Sammed Shikhar Ji in the presence of Acharya Shri?
Answer — In Phalguna of 1928.

**Question 132 —** Who sponsored this Panchakalyanak by his donation?
Answer — Seth Pooran Sah Ji of Seoni.

**Question 133 —** About how many men and women attended the Shikhar Ji Panchakalyanak?
Answer — About more than one lakh men and women from all over India.

**Question 134 —** Where was Acharya Shri's first chaturmas in North India?
Answer — At Katni, district Jabalpur (Madhya Pradesh).

**Question 135 —** Where did Acharya Shri perform the *Simha Nishkridita* vow?
Answer — During the Lalitpur chaturmas.

**Question 136 —** How many times did Acharya Shri perform the *Simha Nishkridita* austerity?
Answer — Three times.

**Question 137 —** How many fasts did Acharya Shri perform in his lifetime?
Answer — 9,938 fasts.

**Question 138 —** Of Acharya Shri's 35 years as a Muni, how many days were spent in fasting?
Answer — 27 years and 2 months were spent in fasting.

**Question 139 —** How many *Muni dikshas* did Acharya Shri confer at Sonagiri?
Answer — Four *Muni dikshas*.

**Question 140 —** On which day did Acharya Shri confer *Muni diksha* at Sonagiri?
Answer — On Agahan (Margashirsha) Shukla Purnima, at nine in the morning.

**Question 141 —** To whom did Acharya Shri give *Muni diksha* at Sonagiri?
Answer — To the four Ailakas Shri Chandrasagar Ji, Payasagar Ji, Parshvakirti Ji and Nemisagar Ji. Parshvakirti Ji was named Muni Kunthusagar, and the others retained the same names.

**Question 142 —** How long did Acharya Shri do *japa* (mantra-recitation) in 24 hours?
Answer — From 9 at night till 7 in the morning; 3 hours at midday; and three hours in the evening.

**Question 143 —** Why did Acharya Shri have people wear the *yajnopavita* (sacred thread)?
Answer — "It is the symbol of the *Ratnatraya* (the Three Jewels). Only he is entitled to give charity and perform worship who has undergone the *yajnopavita samskara*."

**Question 144 —** When Maharaj Ji was asked to accomplish (*siddha*) a mantra, why did he refuse?
Answer — "One with vows (*vrati*) does not bow to one without vows. To accomplish a *vidya* or a deity, bowing is necessary."

**Question 145 —** What hardship (*parishaha*) did Acharya Shri have to bear while going to Badwani?
Answer — In the month of Jyeshtha, after the midday *samayika*, he walked daily on a hot tar road.

**Question 146 —** How did Acharya Shri save the life of Br. Jindas?
Answer — A white mark of leprosy had appeared on his forehead. He wanted to commit suicide. Acharya Shri consoled him and told him to recite the *Ekibhava Stotra* with purity. By that his disease was cured.

**Question 147 —** Where in Delhi did Acharya Shri travel with the Sangha?
Answer — He travelled to New Delhi, the Red Fort, the Jama Masjid, the Viceroy's House and other places.

**Question 148 —** How did Acharya Shri preserve the *Siddhanta* scriptures?
Answer — He had the *Siddhanta* scriptures engraved on copper plates.

**Question 149 —** In which states did Acharya Shri's Sangha travel?
Answer — In Maharashtra, Karnataka, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat and other provinces.

**Question 150 —** Why has Acharya Shri been called *Gurunam Guru* (the Guru of Gurus)?
Answer — Because his *diksha*-guru Devendrasagar Ji, on experiencing faults in his own vows, took *diksha* afresh from Acharya Shri as expiation.

**Question 151 —** How many *japas* of the *Namokar Mantra* did Acharya Shri perform?
Answer — He performed eighteen crore *japas*.

**Question 152 —** When a crisis befell the Dharma, how many *japas* did he perform?
Answer — He performed an *anushthana* of one and a quarter lakh *japas*.

**Question 153 —** Where are the *Siddhanta* scriptures engraved on copper plates?
Answer — In Phaltan town.

**Question 154 —** Which *Siddhanta* scriptures did Acharya Shri have preserved?
Answer — The *Dhavala*, *Jayadhavala* and *Mahadhavala*.

**Question 155 —** When was the Jaipur chaturmas of Acharya Shri's Sangha?
Answer — The fifth chaturmas in Rajasthan was at Jaipur in 1932.

**Question 156 —** What *upasarga* did Acharya Shri face in Jaipur, and where?
Answer — At Khaniya Ji, Jaipur, one day someone shut the door of his room. The passage for air to go out and come in was closed. Acharya Shri fainted from suffocation.

**Question 157 —** When did Acharya Shri renounce grain (*anna*)?
Answer — On 15 August 1948.

**Question 158 —** When did the Bombay High Court's verdict come, and what was it?
Answer — On 24 July 1951 the verdict came that there is a difference in fundamentals between Jains and Hindus. Jain temples cannot be Hindu temples. Hence followers of other religions have no right to enter them without asking.

**Question 159 —** When did Acharya Shri begin taking grain again as food?
Answer — On 16 August 1951, the day of Raksha Bandhan.

**Question 160 —** After how many days did Acharya Shri take grain as food, and where?
Answer — After 1,105 days, in the pandal at Baramati.

**Question 161 —** What did Acharya Shri say the day before he took food?
Answer — "We have no worry about ourselves; but thousands of people have made renunciations on our account. Out of consideration for them, we shall take grain as food tomorrow."

**Question 162 —** What did Acharya Shri say about the poor and the suffering?
Answer — "Remove the distress of the poor and the suffering; give food to the hungry; educate them."

**Question 163 —** When did Acharya Shri go into the forest to meditate?
Answer — He went on the days of Ashtami and Chaturdashi.
