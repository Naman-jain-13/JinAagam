## Kshullaka, Ailaka and Muni Life

**Question 56 —** How long after the *samadhi* (passing) of Satgauda's father did he take *Kshullaka diksha*?
Answer — Four years later.

**Question 57 —** How old was Satgauda when he took *Kshullaka diksha*?
Answer — About forty-one years.

**Question 58 —** After *Kshullaka diksha*, what was he given in place of a *kamandalu* (water-vessel), and where did he obtain a *kamandalu*?
Answer — In place of a *kamandalu* he was given a *lota* (small water pot). In Kagal village a devout shravaka presented him with a *kamandalu*.

**Question 59 —** Can a person of right faith (*samyagdrishti*) take recourse to false (*mithya*) mantras, as one takes medicine, for curing disease?
Answer — Maharaj Ji said: "There is no objection to taking medicine, for in medicine there is neither right faith nor wrong faith. But to accomplish one's purpose through mantras that involve the worship of false deities is to place faith in *mithyatva* (wrong belief); it would be a glorification of *mithyatva*. Therefore one should not take recourse to them."

**Question 60 —** Where did Maharaj have the shravakas remove the idols of false deities (*kudeva*) from their homes?
Answer — In Jainbadi.

**Question 61 —** What was the second incident at Jainbadi?
Answer — A Jain shravaka used to remove snake-poison with *mithya* mantras. The Jain brothers there said: "Do not make him give up his *mithyatva*."

**Question 62 —** What did Maharaj say to the Jain brothers of Jainbadi?
Answer — "We shall give him another mantra." He had him give up *mithyatva* for two months and gave him a mantra.

**Question 63 —** What was the effect of that mantra?
Answer — A bullock had been bitten by a snake. By that mantra the snake's venom was removed at once; and the Jain brother reported that by this mantra other terrible diseases also went away. He gave up *mithyatva* for ever.

**Question 64 —** Where did Kshullaka Shantisagar take *Ailaka diksha*?
Answer — At Girnar Ji, the *nirvana-bhumi* (place of liberation) of Bhagwan Neminath.

**Question 65 —** With whom did Kshullaka Shantisagar Ji go to Girnar Ji?
Answer — With the shravakas of Samdoli.

**Question 66 —** By what means did Kshullaka Shantisagar Ji go to Girnar Ji?
Answer — He went to Girnar Ji by train.

**Question 67 —** Where did Kshullaka Shantisagar Ji renounce vehicles?
Answer — At Kundal station near Sangli he got down from the train, had *darshan* of the temple, and renounced vehicles for ever.

**Question 68 —** At Girnar Ji, why did Ailaka Shantisagar Ji one day go into the *chauka* (place of food-offering) for food and return without taking it?
Answer — A *sadhu* must stop taking food 72 minutes (three *ghadi*) before evening. So little time remained that the food could not have been finished; hence, because of the possibility of a fault in the vow, he returned.

**Question 69 —** Where was Ailaka Shantisagar's first chaturmas?
Answer — In Naslapur.

**Question 70 —** If a *sadhu* does not obtain food on his first round (*charya*), may he go out a second time?
Answer — Having made the resolve to go out for food and then going out twice, the vow of a single meal becomes tainted. Therefore, going out only once — in the morning or after noon — is the path of Dharma.

**Question 71 —** Why did Kshullaka Shantisagar not obtain food for many days?
Answer — In those days the Jain priest (*upadhyaya*) used to fix the place in advance at a householder's home, and the *sadhus* used to go there. In this there arose the fault of *uddishta ahara* (food prepared specifically for the recipient). He (Shantisagar) used to set out for *charya* from the temple. No one knew the (proper) method; therefore for many days he did not obtain food.

**Question 72 —** Where was the *parasmani* (philosopher's stone) in the navel of the image of Bhagwan Parshvanath, and where did it go afterwards?
Answer — In Baba Nagar, an *upadhyaya* used at first, in times of distress, to make gold from it occasionally. After his death, his son made gold in a single day by touching many iron rods to it. Someone (then) took the *paras* away.

**Question 73 —** When did Kshullaka Shantisagar Ji take *Ailaka diksha*?
Answer — On Pausha Shukla 14 (Chaudas).

**Question 74 —** How many times did a snake inflict *upasarga* (affliction) on Shantisagar Ji while he was a Kshullaka?
Answer — Twice.

**Question 75 —** From whom did Shantisagar Ji take *Muni diksha*?
Answer — From Shri Devendra Kirti Ji Maharaj.

**Question 76 —** When did Shantisagar Maharaj take *Muni diksha*?
Answer — On Phalguna Shukla 14 (Chaturdashi), Vikram Samvat 1976, 1920 CE.

**Question 77 —** Where did Shantisagar Maharaj take *Muni diksha*?
Answer — In Yarnal village, Belgaum district, Karnataka province.

**Question 78 —** Where was Muni Shantisagar Maharaj's first chaturmas?
Answer — In Kognoli village.

**Question 79 —** In Kognoli village, who inflicted *upasarga* on Muni Shantisagar Ji, and how?
Answer — A madman, by throwing bricks and stones.

**Question 80 —** Which festival was going on in Yarnal village at the time of Shantisagar Ji's *Muni diksha*?
Answer — The Panchakalyanak Mahotsav of Bhagwan Chandraprabha was being held with great splendour.

**Question 81 —** What happened after Muni Shantisagar Ji's *diksha*?
Answer — Because the water in Yarnal had become contaminated, disease spread; for one month his body became extremely emaciated on account of fever.

**Question 82 —** How was Muni Shantisagar Ji's illness relieved?
Answer — The devout shravakas of Naslapur came and rendered great *vaiyavritya* (service to the ascetic).

**Question 83 —** In Kognoli village, what happened on the way while going for food, and what did he do?
Answer — On the way there was the house of a Brahmin (*vipra*). He objected in abusive language to their passing in front of his house. They changed their route of coming and going.

**Question 84 —** Where was Muni Shantisagar Ji's second chaturmas?
Answer — In Naslapur.

**Question 85 —** What incident took place with Muni Shantisagar Ji at Ainapur?
Answer — A shravaka, by mistake, received him for food (*padgahan*). That day pure food had not been prepared in his house. Very discreetly he brought the food-materials from the neighbouring house and offered the food, not letting Maharaj know.

**Question 86 —** When Shantisagar Ji learnt that the food offered had been brought from another's house, what did he do?
Answer — As expiation (*prayashchitta*), in the month of Jyeshtha, at midday, he went and began meditating on a rock scorched by the fierce rays of the sun.

**Question 87 —** How much older was Devgauda than Satgauda, and what was his name after becoming a Muni?
Answer — He was 9 years and 10 days older, and as a Muni his name was Vardhamansagar Maharaj.

**Question 88 —** How many times did snakes inflict *upasarga* on you (Acharya Shri) during your ascetic life?
Answer — Five times.

**Question 89 —** Where did the greatest *upasarga* by a snake take place?
Answer — In Kognoli (Karnataka).

**Question 90 —** In Kognoli, for how long did the snake inflict *upasarga* on Shantisagar Maharaj, and how large was it?
Answer — It remained coiled around him for two hours, and it was eight feet long.

**Question 91 —** Where was Acharya Shri's chaturmas of 1923, and how many caves were there?
Answer — In Konnur village. There were seven hundred caves there.

**Question 92 —** When Shantisagar Maharaj was made to take food brought from another's house, what else did he do besides meditating on the hot rock?
Answer — For eight days he took nothing in his food except milk.

**Question 93 —** While meditating in the cave at Konnur, who inflicted *upasarga* on Acharya Shri?
Answer — A snake climbed up onto his body.

**Question 94 —** In the Konnur cave, for how long did the snake remain near Acharya Shri, and at what time of day did this occur?
Answer — There the snake remained for three hours, and this happened in the afternoon.

**Question 95 —** When Acharya Shri was asked about the *upasarga* by the snake — "Were you not afraid?" — what did he say?
Answer — "At the time of calamity we never felt fear or panic. We did not even feel the snake, because at that time our attention was fixed on Siddha Bhagwan. The snake came onto the body, coiled itself, and went away. What is there of importance in this?"

**Question 96 —** Where did the *upasarga* by a snake on Kshullaka Shantisagar Maharaj take place?
Answer — In the ancient Jain temple of Kognoli.

**Question 97 —** Where else did a snake inflict *upasarga* on Shantisagar Maharaj?
Answer — In Shedwal village.

**Question 98 —** Where did a large ant (*makoda*) inflict *upasarga* on Shantisagar Maharaj, and how?
Answer — In the forest of Konnur a large ant clung to his male organ and sucked blood; blood was flowing out.

**Question 99 —** For how long did the *upasarga* by the ant continue?
Answer — For an hour and a half.

**Question 100 —** Where did the *upasarga* by ants on Acharya Shantisagar Maharaj take place, and for how long?
Answer — In a temple in the forest, for the whole night.

**Question 101 —** What happened when Acharya Shri's guru Devendra Kirti Ji was coming from Gokak town to Konnur?
Answer — As it took longer than expected, night fell on the way in the forest. After performing the evening *samayika*, both Maharajs — Devendrakirti Ji and Aganya Pandit — went within a line drawn (on the ground). At night a lion came, but it could not enter within that line, and went away.

**Question 102 —** Where else did a snake come upon Acharya Shri?
Answer — While he was meditating in a cave in the forest, a thick snake 12–13 feet long spread its hood and stood before his face.

**Question 103 —** How did the *upasarga* by ants occur?
Answer — Through the carelessness of the priest, while pouring oil into the lamp some oil fell on the ground, whereby ants came, and all night long they kept biting the lower part of Acharya Shri's body, the buttocks etc., and blood began to flow from the body.

**Question 104 —** How many pages of the Agam scriptures did Shantisagar Maharaj study (*swadhyaya*) every day?
Answer — He studied fifty pages.

**Question 105 —** Which subject did Acharya Shri say should be known first?
Answer — "First one should have knowledge of *karma-bandha* (the bondage of karma); not *Samayasara* first."

**Question 106 —** Why did Acharya Shri say that knowledge of *karma-bandha* should come first?
Answer — "Only when there is knowledge of bondage can there be knowledge of *moksha* (liberation). Only when we know for what reason we have fallen into suffering shall we make the effort to escape from it."
