# Aarti of the First Acharya, 108 Charitra Chakravarti Shri Shantisagar Ji Maharaj

*Shantisagar Maharaj, aaj thari aarti utaroon.*
*Aarti utaroon thane pal pal niharoon. Chandra sam deekhe teri kaay — aaj thari aarti utaroon…*

Shantisagar Maharaj, today I perform your *aarti*. I perform your *aarti*; every moment I gaze upon you. Your body shines like the moon — today I perform your *aarti*…

*Satyavati mata ke pyare, Bhimgaud ke raj dulare.*
*Daya-dharma avatar. — Aaj thari aarti utaroon…*
Beloved of mother Satyavati, darling of Bhimgauda; the very embodiment of compassion and Dharma. — Today I perform your *aarti*…

*Munivrat ki diksha hai dhari, charitra rath par kari savari.*
*Syadvad dhwaja phahray. — Aaj thari aarti utaroon…*
You took the *diksha* of the Muni's vows and rode upon the chariot of Conduct, flying the banner of *Syadvada*. — Today I perform your *aarti*…

*Agam-path ke the tum rahi, muni charya aakar batlai.*
*Maha tapasvi Nath! — Aaj thari aarti utaroon…*
You were a traveller on the path of the Agam; you came and taught the conduct of the Muni, O great ascetic Lord! — Today I perform your *aarti*…

*Pavan samaan vicharne wale, roke se na rukne wale.*
*Charitra-chakri raay. — Aaj thari aarti utaroon…*
Wandering like the wind, not to be stopped by any hindrance; King among the Charitra Chakravartis. — Today I perform your *aarti*…

*Sarparaj tan par chadh aaya, Simharaj se bhay nahin khaya.*
*Parishaha sahe apaar. — Aaj thari aarti utaroon…*
The king of serpents climbed upon your body; you felt no fear of the king of lions. You bore boundless hardships. — Today I perform your *aarti*…

*Guruvar tumse kuchh na chahen, dharma marg par hamen lagaven.*
*Shir par rahe tera haath. — Aaj thari aarti utaroon…*
O great Guru, we ask nothing of you but this: set us on the path of Dharma. May your hand remain on our head. — Today I perform your *aarti*…

*Agam mandir aap bachaya, mithyatam ko door bhagaya.*
*Prabhav badhe din raat. — Aaj thari aarti utaroon…*
You saved the Agam and the temples; you drove far away the darkness of *mithyatva*. May Prabhav's welfare grow day and night. — Today I perform your *aarti*…

*Shantisagar Maharaj, aaj thari aarti utaroon…*
*Aarti utaroon thane pal-pal niharoon.*
*Chandra sam deekhe thari kaay… Aaj thari aarti utaroon…*
Shantisagar Maharaj, today I perform your *aarti*… I perform your *aarti*; every moment I gaze upon you. Your body shines like the moon… Today I perform your *aarti*…

*Shri Shanti-Vira-Shiva-Dharmajita-Vardhamana Suribhyo Namah.*
(Salutations to the Acharyas Shanti(sagar), Vira(sagar), Shiva(sagar), Dharma(sagar), Ajita(sagar) and Vardhaman(sagar).)

# Shantisagar Chalisa

*Arihant, Siddha, Acharya Guruvar, Upadhyaya, Sadhu sukhaday.*
*Panch Paramapad manhi viraje, teen lok ke mangalday.*
*Shantisagar Ji Guru hamare, unki mahima kahi na jaay.*
*Chalisa unka jo padhta, sankat uske door palay.*

The Arihant, the Siddha, the Acharya (great Guru), the Upadhyaya and the Sadhu — givers of happiness; they abide in the five Supreme Stations and bestow auspiciousness on the three worlds. Shantisagar Ji is our Guru; his glory cannot be told. Whoever recites his Chalisa — his troubles flee far away.

## Chaupai

*Shanti Guru tum ho hitkari, sab jeevon par karuna dhari.*
*Satyavati mata ke pyare, Bhimgauda ke raj dulare.*
Guru of Peace, you are the benefactor, bearing compassion for all beings; beloved of mother Satyavati, darling of Bhimgauda.

*Mata ko jab sapna aaya, sahasra dalon ke kamal mangaya.*
*Mahapurush ne garbh liya hai, mat-pita Jin pooj kiya hai.*
When the dream came to the mother, she had thousand-petalled lotuses brought. A great soul had entered the womb; the parents performed the worship of the Jina.

*Ashadha badi chhattham shubh aayi, samvat ikanav-untees batayi.*
*Yelgul gram mein janm liya hai, Satgauda Patil naam diya hai.*
The auspicious Ashadha Krishna 6 came — the year is given as (Vikram) Samvat 1929. He was born in Yelgul village and given the name Satgauda Patil.

*Jo bhi muni nagari mein aate, kandhon se nadi paar karate.*
*Shodash varsh ki aayu manhi, aajeevan brahmacharya lahai.*
Whatever Munis came to the town, he carried them across the river on his shoulders. At the age of sixteen he took up lifelong celibacy.

*Phagun sudi chaudas jab aayi, samvat chhiyattar ki batlai.*
*Devguru se diksha payi, muni charya tumne dikhlai.*
When Phalguna Shukla 14 came — of Samvat (19)76, it is told — you received *diksha* from Guru Devendra(kirti); you showed the conduct of a Muni.

*Kognoli ik gaon bataya, pahla varshayog dharaya.*
*Pagal ek vahan par aaya, eenten patthar bahu barsaya.*
A village called Kognoli — there the first chaturmas was held. A madman came there and rained many bricks and stones.

*Zara na man mein khed kiya hai, karuna urmein dhaar liya hai.*
*Jangal mein Jin mandir manhi, achal dhyan dhara Munirai.*
Not the least distress did you feel in your mind; you held compassion in your heart. In a Jain temple in the forest the Muni-king held unshakeable meditation.

*Cheenti ne upasarg kiya hai, sare tan ko kaat liya hai.*
*To bhi nek dige nahin Swami, samata ke sagar tum naami.*
Ants inflicted affliction; they bit the whole body. Even so you did not waver in the least, O Master, renowned as the ocean of equanimity.

*Nagraj upasarg kiya hai, tan par aakar lipat gaya hai.*
*Man mein khed zara nahin aana, Shantisagar daya-nidhana.*
The king of serpents inflicted affliction; it came and coiled around your body. Not the least distress entered your mind, O Shantisagar, treasure of compassion.

*Ashwin sudi ekadash aayi, samvat ik assi batlai.*
*Samdoli ik gaon navina, Sangh ne Acharaj pad deena.*
Ashwina Shukla 11 came — of Samvat (19)81, it is told. In a new village, Samdoli, the Sangha gave you the Acharya Pada.

*Veer, Nemi ne diksha payi, teen lok mein khushiyan chhayi.*
*Gajpantha Siddhakshetra bataya, Charitra Chakravarti pad paya.*
Veer(sagar) and Nemi(sagar) received *diksha*; joy spread over the three worlds. At Gajpantha Siddhakshetra, it is told, you received the title Charitra Chakravarti.

*Gokak ki ik gupha batayi, nishi mein dhyan dhara Munirai.*
*Nahar ek vahan par aaya, Yam se bhi tum bhay nahin khaya.*
In a cave at Gokak, it is told, the Muni-king held meditation at night. A lion came there; you were not afraid even of Yama (Death).

*Kai jagah par vanachar aate, aakar tumko sheesh namate.*
*Rajakheda gram bataya, Chhiddilal vahan par aaya.*
In many places wild creatures came and bowed their heads to you. At Rajkheda village, it is told, Chhiddilal came there.

*Munihatya karne ki thani, pakda gaya vahan par mani.*
*Usko tumne turat chhudaya, kshamasheel ka dharma nibhaya.*
He had resolved to murder the Muni; he was caught there and confessed. You had him released at once; you fulfilled the Dharma of the forgiving.

*Kai dinon tak jal nahin paya, trishna garmi khoob sataya.*
*Samatadhari thi man manhi, Shanti ke sagar Gururai.*
For many days you got no water; thirst and heat tormented you greatly. Equanimity was in your mind, O Guru-king, Ocean of Peace.

*Nar, tiryanch, devkrit bhai, pudgal milkar chaar batayi.*
*Charon hi upasarg sahe hain, zara na man mein khed lahe hain.*
Caused by men, by animals, by gods — and with matter (*pudgala*), four are told. You bore all four kinds of affliction; not the least distress arose in your mind.

*Tap kar Swami karm khipaye, vaisa tap ham bhi kar paaven.*
*Bina Guru ke ham rahte the, anyamati "nagura" kahte the.*
By austerity, O Master, you wore away the karmas — may we too be able to perform such austerity. We were living without a Guru; those of other faiths called us "guru-less."

*Uttar mein to ek nahin the, Dakshin mein bhi kahin kahin the.*
*Aakar tumne marg dikhaya, muni-charya ka gyan karaya.*
In the North there was not even one (Muni); in the South too there were only a few here and there. You came and showed the path; you gave knowledge of the Muni's conduct.

*Uttar mein jo tum na jate, muni charya ko kaun batate?*
*Agam ke anukool bataya, kahin na usmein avgun paya.*
Had you not gone to the North, who would have taught the Muni's conduct? You taught in accordance with the Agam; no fault was found in it anywhere.

*Jinmandir par sankat aaya, Guruvar tumne use bachaya.*
*Jinavani ki raksha keeni, tamrapatra par likhva deeni.*
When a crisis came upon the Jain temples, O great Guru, you saved them. You protected the *Jinavani*; you had it written on copper plates.

*Indriya panchon vash mein keeni, chhahon kaay ki raksha keeni.*
*Simha samaan maha baldhari, hathi sam the unnat bhari.*
You brought the five senses under control; you protected the six classes of beings. Mighty in strength like a lion, lofty and grand like an elephant.

*Pavan samaan vicharne wale, roke se nahin rukne wale.*
*Meru sam tha dhyan tumhara, karma shatru bhi tumse hara.*
Wandering like the wind, not to be stopped by any hindrance. Your meditation was like Meru; even the enemy karma was defeated by you.

*Surya samaan tej batlaya, chandra kiran sam sheetal paya.*
*Urag samaan aashray na paya, apna aashram nahin banaya.*
Your brilliance is told to be like the sun; your coolness was found to be like the rays of the moon. Like a serpent you took no fixed shelter; you built no *ashram* of your own.

*Rishabh samaan bhadra parinami, kirti phaili jag mein naami.*
*Chetan mein hi raman kiya hai, parbhavon ko chhod diya hai.*
Gentle in disposition like Rishabha; your renowned fame spread through the world. You delighted only in the conscious Self; you abandoned all alien states.

*Agam praan aapke manhi, aap bane Shivpur ke rahi.*
*Poonam Ghasilal bataye, charanan aakar sheesh namaye.*
The Agam is the very life within you; you became a traveller to the City of Liberation. Poonam(chand) Ghasilal, it is told, came and bowed his head at your feet.

*Aakar Guru se vinati keeni, Shikhargiri ki vandan keeni.*
*Dakshin mein mithya mat chhaya, mithya mat ka tyag karaya.*
He came and made a request to the Guru; the *vandana* of Shikhar mountain was performed. In the South false belief prevailed; you had false belief renounced.

*Uttar mein jab Sangh gaya hai, shithilachar ko mita diya hai.*
*Goonga bole, langda chale, kushti apna kusht mita le.*
When the Sangha went to the North, laxity of conduct was wiped out. The dumb spoke, the lame walked, the leper had his leprosy removed.

*Jo bhi tav charanon mein aaya, sankat uska turat mitaya.*
Whoever came to your feet — his trouble was removed at once.

**Doha:**
*Path kare chalis din, Guru ka lekar naam.*
*"Prabhav" badhe din raat hi, Shivpur mein ho dhaam.*
Whoever recites this for forty days, taking the Guru's name — may his welfare (*prabhav*) grow day and night; may his abode be in the City of Liberation.
