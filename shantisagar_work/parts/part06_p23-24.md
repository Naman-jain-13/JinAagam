**Question 164 —** What did Acharya Shri say about an untoward event, and what untoward event occurred?
Answer — Acharya Shri had said that some calamity would soon befall the country; just four days later Gandhi Ji was assassinated.

**Question 165 —** For how many days in succession did Acharya Shri not receive water in his food?
Answer — For ten days in succession he did not receive water.

**Question 166 —** Who made an attempt on the lives of the Acharya Sangha, where and when?
Answer — A person named Chhidi did so in Rajkheda village, on 11 February 1930.

**Question 167 —** What directions did Acharya Shri give to society?
Answer — He directed the opening of *gurukulas*, educational institutions, Jain *pathshalas*, orphanages, *Jinavani* (scripture) publishing institutions, and the like; and the prohibition of remarriage (of widows), inter-caste marriage and child marriage, etc.

**Question 168 —** Where and how did the *samadhi* of Anant Kirti Maharaj occur?
Answer — In Morena. It was the cold season. A devotee placed a burning brazier (*sigadi*) in his room and shut the room. Maharaj had epilepsy; he fell down unconscious and his foot went into the brazier. The blood that flowed from the foot put out the fire, but a few days later he attained *samadhi*.

**Question 169 —** What is the rule (*shasana*) of the Acharya Parameshthi like?
Answer — It is the rule of the Vitaraga (the Passionless One).

**Question 170 —** What is the life-breath (*prana*) of the *sadhus*?
Answer — The Agam.

**Question 171 —** How many *Muni dikshas* did Acharya Shri confer?
Answer — 26 *Muni dikshas*.

**Question 172 —** Name the *nirgrantha* Muni disciples of Acharya Shri.
Answer — 108 Muni Shri Veersagar Ji, Nemisagar Ji [Nemappa Ji (of Kudchi)], Chandrasagar Ji, Payasagar Ji, Nemisagar Ji (of Puntur), Kunthusagar Ji, Dharmasagar Ji, Sudharmasagar Ji, Adisagar Ji and Vardhamansagar Ji.

**Question 173 —** To whom did Acharya Shri give the first *Aryika diksha*?
Answer — To Chandramati Mataji.

**Question 174 —** How many *Kshullaka dikshas* did Acharya Shri confer?
Answer — 28 *Kshullaka dikshas*.

**Question 175 —** How many *Kshullika dikshas* did Acharya Shri confer?
Answer — He conferred 14 *Kshullika dikshas*.

**Question 176 —** How many chaturmas did Acharya Shri complete?
Answer — In all, 41 chaturmas.

**Question 177 —** Where was Acharya Shri's last chaturmas?
Answer — At Kunthalgiri Siddhakshetra.

**Question 178 —** Where did Acharya Shri adopt the twelve-year Utkrishta Sallekhana, and on which day?
Answer — At Gajpantha Siddhakshetra, on the day of Vijaya Dashami.

**Question 179 —** To which 92-year-old Muni did Acharya Shri give Sallekhana?
Answer — To Muni Vardhamansagar Ji, his elder brother from householder life.

**Question 180 —** To whom did Acharya Shri give his own Acharya Pada?
Answer — To his first Muni disciple, Shri Veersagar Ji Maharaj.

**Question 181 —** What message did Acharya Shri send to Muni Veersagar Ji?
Answer — "Conduct yourself according to the Agam; attain *samadhimarana* as we have; and make a worthy disciple your successor."

**Question 182 —** For how many days did Acharya Shri's final Sallekhana continue?
Answer — For 35 days; *samadhi* occurred on the 36th day.

**Question 183 —** When did Acharya Shri give the Acharya Pada to Veersagar Ji?
Answer — On 26 August 1955, a Friday, by letter.

**Question 184 —** Who wrote the letter, and what was he made to write in it?
Answer — Pandit Sumeruchandra Diwakar wrote it. Maharaj had him write: "Of our own accord and with satisfaction, we confer the Acharya Pada upon our first *nirgrantha* disciple, Veersagar."

**Question 185 —** When did Acharya Shri take *Yama Sallekhana* (Sallekhana unto death)?
Answer — On 14 August 1955.

**Question 186 —** When did Acharya Shri give his last discourse?
Answer — On the 26th day of the Sallekhana, 6 September 1955, in the Marathi language.

**Question 187 —** For how long did Acharya Shri give his last discourse?
Answer — For 22 minutes.

**Question 188 —** At what age did Acharya Shri attain *samadhi*?
Answer — At the age of 84.

**Question 189 —** When and where did Acharya Shri attain *samadhi*?
Answer — At Kunthalgiri Siddhakshetra on 18 September 1955, Bhadrapada Shukla 2, at 6:50 in the morning.

**Question 190 —** In his last discourse, what instruction did Acharya Shri give to those desiring happiness?
Answer — "Destroy *Darshana Mohaniya* (faith-deluding karma) and attain *Samyaktva* (right faith)."

**Question 191 —** What did Acharya Shri say about *samyama* (self-restraint) in his discourse?
Answer — "Do not fear *samyama*; adopt *samyama*. Without it there will be no welfare. Adopt *samyama* to destroy *Charitra Mohaniya* (conduct-deluding karma). Instead of going to hell you will at least go to heaven."

**Question 192 —** What instruction was given about *ahimsa* (non-violence), and what is the root of the Jain Dharma?
Answer — "The root of Dharma is compassion, *ahimsa*. Practise truth and *ahimsa*. Work is not accomplished by saying it with the mouth; the fruit is obtained by doing."
