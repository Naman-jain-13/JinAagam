## Arghya to Abhikshna Gyanopayogi (Ever Engaged in Knowledge) Acharya 108 Shri Ajitsagar Ji Maharaj

*Kaal anadi bhatke jag mein, raah kahin na payi hai.*
*Shivpur ki ab raah batado, charanan pooj rachai hai.*
*Bal brahmachari ho Guruvar, Kamdev ko mara hai.*
*Gyan dhyan tap leen tapasvi, Dharmasindhu patta dhara hai.*

From beginningless time we have wandered in the world; nowhere have we found the path. Show us now the road to the City of Liberation — we have composed this worship at your feet. A celibate from childhood, O great Guru, you have slain Kamadeva. An ascetic absorbed in knowledge, meditation and austerity, you have held the seat (*patta*) of the Ocean of Dharma (Dharmasagar).

*Om Hreem Abhikshna Gyanopayogi 108 Acharya Shri Ajitsagar Ji argham nirvapamiti svaha.*

## Arghya to Acharya-kalpa 108 Shri Shrutsagar Ji Maharaj

*Teen lok ke sab phal khaye, Shiv phal ab tak paya nay.*
*Maha moksha-phal pane karan, argh chadhaoon Guru ke aay.*
*Veer Sindhu ke antim munivar, Shrutsagar Gurudev mahan.*
*Khyati laabh se door rahe ho, nahin liya Suri padmaan.*

I have tasted all the fruits of the three worlds, but the fruit of Liberation I have not yet obtained. To gain the great fruit of *moksha*, I come and offer *arghya* to the Guru. Last of the Munis of the Ocean of Veer (Veersagar), great Gurudev Shrutsagar — you have stayed far from fame and gain; you did not accept the honour of the Acharya (*Suri*) Pada.

*Om Hreem Acharya-kalpa 108 Shri Shrutsagar Ji argham nirvapamiti svaha.*

# Pooja of 108 Acharya Shri Vardhamansagar Maharaj

## Sthapana (Invocation)

*Panchachar ka palan karte, shishyon se karvate hain.*
*Agam path par chale nirantar, bhavsagar tirvate ho.*
*Poorab, Pashchim, Uttar, Dakshin, vrish phailaya jakar ke.*
*Aahvanan ham karen aapka, hriday virajo aakar ke.*

You observe the fivefold conduct and have your disciples observe it. You walk continually on the path of the Agam and carry us across the ocean of existence. Going to the East, West, North and South, you have spread the Dharma (*vrisha*). We invoke you; come and be seated in our heart.

*Om Hreem Shattrimshat-Gunamandita-Acharya Vatsalya Varidhi Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo atra avatara avatara samvaushat — aahvananam.* (Invocation: "O Acharya adorned with the thirty-six virtues, Ocean of Affection, Shri Vardhamansagar, foremost of great ascetics, descend here, descend here.")
*Om Hreem Shattrimshat-Gunamandita-Acharya Vatsalya Varidhi Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo atra tishtha tishtha thah thah — sthapanam.*
*Om Hreem Shattrimshat-Gunamandita-Acharya Vatsalya Varidhi Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo atra mama sannihito bhava bhava vashat — sannidhikaranam.*

## Ashtaka (The Eight Offerings)

**Jala (Water)**
*Prasuk karke jal le aaye, kanchan kalasha bharayenge.*
*Guru charanon mein dhara dekar, janam-maran nashayenge.*
*Path bhoole hain raah batado, pancham patta dhari ho.*
*Vartaman ke Vardhaman ko, shat shat naman hamaro ho.*

Making the water pure (*prasuka*) we have brought it and filled a golden *kalasha*. Pouring a stream at the Guru's feet, we shall destroy birth and death. We have lost the path — show us the way, O holder of the fifth seat (*patta*). To the Vardhaman of the present age, our salutations a hundred times over.

*Om Hreem Shattrimshat-Gunamandita-Acharya Vatsalya Varidhi Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo janma-jara-mrityu-vinashanaya jalam nirvapamiti svaha.*

**Chandana (Sandal)**
*Malayagiri ka chandan lekar, kesar sang ghisayenge.*
*Guru charanon ki pooj rachakar, bhav ka taap mitayenge. — Path bhoole hain…*

Taking sandal of the Malaya mountain, we shall grind it with saffron. Composing the worship of the Guru's feet, we shall remove the heat of *samsara*. — We have lost the path…

*Om Hreem … Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo samsara-taapa-vinashanaya chandanam nirvapamiti svaha.*

**Akshata (Unbroken rice)**
*Chandra-kiran sam shwet manohar, akshat thaal bharayenge.*
*Akshaya-pad ke prapt karan ko, Guru charanon mein layenge. — Path bhoole hain…*

We shall fill a plate with unbroken rice, white and lovely as moonbeams. To attain the imperishable state, we shall bring it to the Guru's feet. — We have lost the path…

*Om Hreem … Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo akshaya-pada-praptaye akshatan nirvapamiti svaha.*

**Pushpa (Flowers)**
*Kamal ketaki pushpa manohar, maha sugandhit laye hain.*
*Kamabaan ka naash karado, charanan bhent chadhaye hain. — Path bhoole hain…*

Lovely lotus and *ketaki* flowers, greatly fragrant, we have brought. Destroy the arrows of desire — we have offered them as a gift at your feet. — We have lost the path…

*Om Hreem … Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo kamabana-vidhvamsanaya pushpam nirvapamiti svaha.*

**Naivedya (Food)**
*Ghevar pheni modak khaja, shuddh banakar laye hain.*
*Kshudha dakini naash karan ko, teri pooj rachaye hain. — Path bhoole hain…*

*Ghevar*, *pheni*, *modak* and *khaja* (sweets), prepared pure, we have brought. To destroy the demoness of hunger, we have composed your worship. — We have lost the path…

*Om Hreem … Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo kshudha-roga-vinashanaya naivedyam nirvapamiti svaha.*

**Deepa (Lamp)**
*Manimay deep jalakar laye, aarti karne aaye hain.*
*Moha mahatam naash karado, yahi bhavna bhaye hain. — Path bhoole hain…*

We have brought a lit jewelled lamp; we have come to perform *aarti*. Destroy the great darkness of delusion — this alone is the feeling we cherish. — We have lost the path…

*Om Hreem … Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo mohandhakara-vinashanaya deepam nirvapamiti svaha.*

**Dhoopa (Incense)**
*Das vidhi dhoop sugandhit lekar, agni manhi jalate hain.*
*Asht karm ki dhoom udado, tav charanon mein aate hain. — Path bhoole hain…*

Taking fragrant ten-fold incense, we burn it in the fire. Send up the smoke of the eight karmas — we come to your feet. — We have lost the path…

*Om Hreem … Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo ashtakarma-dahanaya dhoopam nirvapamiti svaha.*

**Phala (Fruit)**
*Seb santra dadim kela, phal vivarjit laye hain.*
*Shivpur ki Guru raah dikhado, charanan pooj rachaye hain. — Path bhoole hain…*

Apple, orange, pomegranate and banana — we have brought choice fruits. Show us, Guru, the road to the City of Liberation — we have composed the worship of your feet. — We have lost the path…

*Om Hreem … Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo mahamoksha-phala-praptaye phalam nirvapamiti svaha.*

**Arghya (Combined offering)**
*Jal phal aadik asht dravya le, kanchan thaal bharaye hain.*
*Maha moksha phal prapt karan ko, charan kamal mein aaye hain. — Path bhoole hain…*

Taking the eight substances — water, fruit and the rest — we have filled a golden plate. To obtain the great fruit of *moksha*, we have come to your lotus feet. — We have lost the path…

*Om Hreem … Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo anargha-pada-praptaye arghyam nirvapamiti svaha.*

**Shanti-dhara**
*Shantidhara ham karen, man mein ati harshaye.*
*Rog shok sankat mite, janam saphal ho jaye.* — *Shantaye Shantidhara.*

We pour the stream of peace, with great joy in our hearts. May disease, sorrow and calamity vanish; may this birth become fruitful.

**Pushpanjali**
*Nana vidhi ke suman le, sundar maal banay.*
*Pushpanjali arpan kare, kamabaan nash jaay.* — *Pushpanjali.*

Taking flowers of many kinds, making a beautiful garland, we offer the handful of flowers; may the arrows of desire be destroyed.

## Jayamala

**Doha:**
*Guru ki mahima agam hai, kaise karoon bakhan.*
*Bhakti ke vash ho raha, karta hoon yashgaan.*
The glory of the Guru is unfathomable; how shall I describe it? Overcome by devotion, I sing his praise.

*Yashwant bankar janm liya tha, ab yah bane yashasvi sant.*
*Nikal pade hain karm kaatne, karne ko bhav ka ab ant. ॥1॥*
He was born as Yashwant; now he has become a renowned saint. He has set out to cut the karmas, to make an end of worldly existence now. (1)

*Bhadav Shukla saate shubh din, Maha Sanavad nagar manjhar.*
*Kamalchand Ji pita aapke, ghar-ghar ho raha mangalachar.*
*Maa Manorama ke sut pyare, naam dharaya tha Yashwant.*
*Nikal pade hain karm kaatne… ॥2॥*
On the auspicious day of Bhadrapada Shukla 7, in the town of Maha Sanavad — Kamalchand Ji was your father; in every home there were auspicious rites. Beloved son of mother Manorama, you were given the name Yashwant. He has set out to cut the karmas… (2)

*Ashtadash ki bhai umra jab, aaye Shivsagar ke paas.*
*Diksha ki arji kar deeni, charanon mein aaya hai daas.*
*Shikhar Sammed Guru ne bheja, peechhe ho gaya Shiv ka ant.*
*Nikal pade hain karm kaatne… ॥3॥*
When he reached the age of eighteen, he came to Shivsagar. He made his petition for *diksha*: "This servant has come to your feet." The Guru sent him to Shikhar Sammed; afterwards Shiv(sagar)'s end came. He has set out to cut the karmas… (3)

*Dharma Sindhu se diksha leeni, parishaha sahe maha ghanghor.*
*Nainan jyoti chali gayi hai, chalta nahin kisi ka jor.*
*Shanti Prabhu ki bhakti keeni, nainan jyoti payi sant.*
*Nikal pade hain karm kaatne… ॥4॥*
He took *diksha* from the Ocean of Dharma (Dharmasagar); he bore hardships most terrible. The light of his eyes went away; no one's power could avail. He performed the devotion of Lord Shanti(nath); the saint regained the light of his eyes. He has set out to cut the karmas… (4)

*Ajit Suri ne patra likha hai, apna pattadhish banay.*
*Nar-nari sadhu bahu aaye, pancham patta-dhish kahay.*
*Nagar Parsola ke andar, sare sangh ke bane mahant.*
*Nikal pade hain karm kaatne… ॥5॥*
Acharya Ajit(sagar) wrote a letter making him his successor on the seat. Men, women and many *sadhus* came; he was called the fifth holder of the seat. Within the town of Parsola he became the head of the whole Sangha. He has set out to cut the karmas… (5)

*Pancha-mahavrat pancha-samiti, teen gupti palan karte.*
*Baees parishaha sahe nirantar, das-o-dharam dharan karte.*
*Barah bhavan bhave harpal, karam aavate roke sant.*
*Nikal pade hain karm kaatne… ॥6॥*
He observes the five great vows, the five carefulnesses and the three restraints; he bears the twenty-two hardships continually and holds the ten Dharmas. Every moment he contemplates the twelve reflections; the saint stops the inflow of karma. He has set out to cut the karmas… (6)

*Man indriya ko bas mein karli, chhahon kaay par raksha aay.*
*Panchachar ka palan karte, shishyon se palan karvay.*
*Dukhiya jo charanon mein aaya, sankat uska hara turant.*
*Nikal pade hain karm kaatne… ॥7॥*
He has brought mind and senses under control, protecting the six classes of beings. He observes the fivefold conduct and has his disciples observe it. Whoever came in distress to his feet — his trouble was removed at once. He has set out to cut the karmas… (7)

*Bahubali ke Bhattarak Ji, vinati aakar keeni hai.*
*Teer baar abhishek karaye, charanon drishti deeni hai.*
*Nana sangh vahan par aaye, sabhi sanghon ke bane mahant.*
*Nikal pade hain karm kaatne… ॥8॥*
The Bhattarak Ji of Bahubali came and made a request; three times he had the *abhisheka* performed, and cast his gaze upon (the Guru's) feet. Many Sanghas came there; he became the head of all the Sanghas. He has set out to cut the karmas… (8)

*Sara Bharat bhraman kiya hai, nahin banai apni thaur.*
*Nagar-nagar mein ghoom-ghoom kar, yash phailaya charon or.*
*Agam path par chale nirantar, nahin chalaya apna panth.*
*Nikal pade hain karm kaatne… ॥9॥*
He has wandered all over India, never making a place his own. Going from town to town he spread his fame in all four directions. He walked continually on the path of the Agam; he never started a sect of his own. He has set out to cut the karmas… (9)

*Simha samaan maha balshali, hathi sam ho unnat aap.*
*Surya kiran sam tej-punj ho, bhavya jeevon ka harte paap.*
*Meru sam hai dhyan aapka, karam-bali ka kar rahe ant.*
*Nikal pade hain karm kaatne… ॥10॥*
Mighty in strength like a lion, lofty like an elephant; a mass of brilliance like the rays of the sun, removing the sins of *bhavya* souls. Your meditation is like Mount Meru; you are making an end of the mighty karma. He has set out to cut the karmas… (10)

*Pavan samaan vicharne wale, chandra kiran sam sheetal jaan.*
*Baal samaan bhadra parinami, sagar sam gambhir mahan.*
*"Prabhav" badhe din raat aapka, aap hamare ho Bhagwant.*
*Nikal pade hain karm kaatne… ॥11॥*
Wandering like the wind, cool as the rays of the moon; innocent in disposition like a child, deep and great like the ocean. May your influence (*prabhav*) grow day and night; you are our Lord. He has set out to cut the karmas… (11)

*Teen lok kagaj karoon, lekhani sab vanaray.*
*Sab sagar syahi karoon, Guru gun likha na jaay.*
Were I to make the three worlds my paper, all the forests my pens, and all the oceans my ink — still the virtues of the Guru could not be written.

*Om Hreem Shattrimshat-Gunamandita-Acharya Vatsalya Varidhi Shri Vardhamansagar Mahayativarebhyo jayamala poornargham nirvapamiti svaha.*

*Guruvar tere naam se, bigde bante kaam.*
*Prabhav hit badhta rahe, Shivpur mein ho dhaam.*
O great Guru, by your name ruined works are set right. May the welfare of Prabhav keep increasing; may his abode be in the City of Liberation.

*Ityashirvadah — divya pushpanjali.* (Thus the benediction — the divine handful of flowers.)
