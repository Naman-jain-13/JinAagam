# The Twelve Bhavanas (Reflections)

*Sheesh nava Arihant ko, Siddhan karoon pranam,*
*Vardhaman Acharya ka, le sukhakari naam.*
*Upadhyaya sarva-sadhu va, Jinavani ur dhaar,*
*Varnoon barah bhavana, jag jeevan hitkaar.*

Bowing my head to the Arihants, I salute the Siddhas, taking the happiness-giving name of Acharya Vardhaman. Holding in my heart the Upadhyayas, all the Sadhus and the *Jinavani*, I describe the twelve reflections, for the good of the beings of the world.

## Prelude

*Yah samsar athir bhavi jano, jag mein sharana hai na koy.*
*Panch paravartan nit karte, aatam ek akela hoy.*
Know, O *bhavya*, this world to be unstable; in the world there is no refuge. Ever making the five cycles of transmigration, the soul is one and alone.

*Pudgal se hai bhinn chetana, maha apavan hai yah kaay.*
*Aasrav bandh nirantar karta, samvar roke karo upay.*
Consciousness is distinct from matter; this body is utterly impure. It continually causes inflow and bondage — make the effort to stop it by *samvara*.

*Tap se karma nirjara hoti, purushakar lok pahichan.*
*Durlabh hai nar bhav ka pana, daya dharma ko man mein aan.*
Through austerity comes the shedding of karma; recognise the universe as shaped like a standing man. Rare it is to obtain human birth; bring the Dharma of compassion into your mind.

*Moh neend se uthre chetan, aaye Guru jagane ko.*
*Bhavijan barah bhavan bhavo, vitaragata pane ko.*
Rise, O conscious one, from the sleep of delusion — the Guru has come to awaken you. O *bhavya* people, contemplate the twelve reflections, to attain freedom from passion.

## 1. Anitya Bhavana — Impermanence

*Indra sampada vaibhav sare, dhan yauvan parijan nari.*
*Indriya bhog rajbal aagya, shatkhand ke the adhikari.*
The wealth and all the splendour of Indra; riches, youth, kin and wife; sense-pleasures, royal power and command — (even) those who were lords of the six continents —

*Koi nitya nahin is jag mein, kshan bhar mein nashvar hote.*
*Chanchal Lakshmi thir nahin rahti, indrajaal sam yah hote.*
nothing in this world is eternal; in a moment they perish. Fickle Lakshmi does not stay; all this is like a magician's illusion.

*Jo bhi kaal nikal jata hai, laut na vapis aata hai.*
*Jitni aayu beet gayi hai, pal na phir se pata hai.*
Whatever time passes does not return. Of the life that has gone by, not one moment is regained.

*Paap punya jo kiya hai toone, vahi saath mein jayega.*
*Aatma-swabhav sadhana kar lo, yahi thir rah payega.*
Whatever sin and merit you have done — that alone will go with you. Practise the nature of the Self; that alone will remain stable.

## 2. Asharana Bhavana — Refugelessness

*Aayu karma ne jag jeevon ko, pakad rakha hai bahu bhari.*
*Uske mukh mein phanse hue ko, bacha na sakta baldhari.*
The life-span karma holds the beings of the world in a mighty grip. One caught in its mouth — no strong one can save him.

*Mantra yantra sena bahu sari, kaam na tere aayegi.*
*Kaalbali ka lage tamacha, kanth pakad le jayegi.*
Spells, charms, vast armies — none will be of use to you. When the blow of mighty Death lands, it will seize you by the throat and carry you off.

*Chakra-ratna Haladhar sa bhai, haar gaya iske aage.*
*Ek teer ke lagat Krishna ke, praan pakheroo le bhage.*
The Discus-jewel, a brother like Haladhara (Balarama) — all were defeated before it. At the striking of a single arrow, the bird of Krishna's life flew away.

*Arihant Siddha Sadhu is jag mein, Dharma ek sharanagat hai.*
*Iska sharana le lo chetan, janam maran mitavat hai.*
In this world only the Arihant, the Siddha, the Sadhu and the Dharma are refuge. Take their refuge, O conscious one; it wipes out birth and death.

## 3. Samsara Bhavana — The Cycle of Existence

*Mithya-mad maya ke vash ho, is jag mein bahu bhatak raha.*
*Dravya, kshetra, aru kaal, bhav bhav, parivartan mein atak raha.*
Under the sway of false pride and delusion, you have wandered much in this world — stuck in the cycles of substance, place, time, birth and state.

*Nar tiryanch naraki bankar, toone bahu dukh paya hai.*
*Devgati mein trishna ke vash, ekendriya ban aaya hai.*
Becoming human, animal and hell-being you have suffered much; in the celestial state, under the sway of craving, you have come back as a one-sensed being.

*Yah bhav van hai maha bhayanak, bhramte chhor na paya hai.*
*Teen lok mein jitne pudgal, sabko maine khaya hai.*
This forest of existence is most terrible; wandering, you have found no end. All the matter in the three worlds — I have consumed it all.

*Koi jagah bachi na khali, jahan na maine janm liya.*
*Aisa kaal bacha na koi, jab na maine maran kiya.*
No place has been left empty where I have not taken birth; no moment of time has been left in which I have not died.

## 4. Ekatva Bhavana — Solitariness

*Ek akela aaya jag mein, ek akela jayega.*
*Dhan daulat aru maal khazana, yahin pada rah jayega.*
Alone one comes into the world; alone one will go. Wealth, riches, goods and treasure — all will be left lying here.

*Maat pita aru bhai bandhu, swarth ka sab nata hai.*
*Matlab ke sab saathi jag mein, koi saath na jata hai.*
Mother, father, brothers and kin — all are ties of self-interest. In the world all companions are for their own ends; no one goes along with you.

*Sukh mein sare saath tumhare, dukh mein ek na aata hai.*
*Sukh-dukh koi baant na sakta, ek akela pata hai.*
In happiness all are with you; in sorrow not one comes. No one can share your joy and sorrow; alone one experiences them.

*Parjan purjan kutumb kabila, jagah-jagah se aate hain.*
*Apna-apna swarth sadhakar, anya bhavon ko jate hain.*
Kinsmen, townsmen, family and clan come from place to place; having served each his own interest, they go on to other births.

## 5. Anyatva Bhavana — Otherness

*Mrigatrishna sam bhog jagat mein, mithya jal hai chamak raha.*
*Kaal anadi se yah praani, bhav van mein hai bhatak raha.*
Like a mirage, the pleasures in the world are false water glittering; from beginningless time this creature has wandered in the forest of existence.

*Yah mere hain, main hoon inka, ab tak aisi mani hai.*
*Tu chetan aur deh achetan, yah jad tu to gyani hai.*
"These are mine; I am theirs" — so you have believed until now. You are conscious and the body is unconscious; it is inert, you are the knower.

*Teen lok ke jitne chetan, sabse nata joda hai.*
*Ab tak koi hua na tera, sabne munh ko moda hai.*
With every conscious being in the three worlds you have formed a bond; till now none has become yours — all have turned their faces away.

*Ab to jaag sayane chetan, par parinati ko door karo.*
*Mithya bhav hatao praani, samyagdarshan poor karo.*
Now awake, O wise conscious one; put away the modes of the "other." Remove false belief, O creature; fill yourself with right faith.

## 6. Ashuchi Bhavana — Impurity

*Maat-pita raj-veeraj milkar, bani hui hai pyari deh.*
*Mal mootra se bhari kaay mein, nahak karta praani neh.*
From the union of the mother's and father's seed this dear body is formed. In a body full of filth and urine the creature places affection in vain.

*Haad-maans nas lahu raadh bhi, ismein sare batlaye.*
*Chaam lapeti sohe oopar, andar dekhat ghin aave.*
Bone, flesh, sinew, blood and pus — all are said to be in it. Wrapped in skin it looks fine on the outside; looking within, disgust arises.

*Jiska karan hi ashuddh ho, kaise pavan hovegi.*
*Ganga, Yamuna, Sindhu sarita, iska mail na dhovegi.*
That whose very cause is impure — how can it become pure? The Ganga, the Yamuna, the Sindhu rivers — none will wash away its filth.

*Vrat-tap-samyam dharan kar lo, jisse pavan ho jaave.*
*Dharma dhyan mein ise laga do, phale anante phal paave.*
Take up vows, austerity and restraint, by which it may become pure. Engage it in the meditation of Dharma; it will bear fruit and yield infinite fruit.

## 7. Asrava Bhavana — Inflow of Karma

*Man vach kaay yog ke karan, karma aasrav hota hai.*
*Mithya avirati aru kashay se, beej paap ke bota hai.*
Because of the activity of mind, speech and body, the inflow of karma takes place. Through false belief, non-restraint and the passions one sows the seeds of sin.

*Yog nimantran dete vidhi ko, hai kashay manuhar kare.*
*Jaisa bhav shubhashubh karta, vaisa hi to bandh dhare.*
The activities issue the invitation to karma; the passions entertain it. As one's disposition is — good or evil — so is the bondage one takes on.

*Hanste-hanste karma bandhta, uday samay phir rota hai.*
*Paap bandh mein jo rota hai, vah gyani jan hota hai.*
Laughing, one binds karma; at the time of its fruition one weeps. He who weeps at the binding of sin — he is the wise one.

*Aisa yatna karo bhavi praani, aasrav hona ruk jave.*
*Vrat-tap-samyam dharan karke, karma bandh se bach jave.*
Make such effort, O *bhavya* creature, that the inflow may stop; taking up vows, austerity and restraint, escape from the bondage of karma.

## 8. Samvara Bhavana — Stoppage of Inflow

*Jab mori mein daat laga de, jal ka aana ruk jata.*
*Jyon aasrav ko roke samvar, aatam kyon nahin chitlata.*
When a plug is put in the drain, the entry of water stops. Just so does *samvara* stop the inflow — why, O soul, do you not reflect on this?

*Pancha-mahavrat pancha-samiti, teen-gupti ko jo paale.*
*Das-vidh dharma parishaha baees, barah bhavan ko bhaale.*
He who observes the five great vows, the five carefulnesses and the three restraints, who cherishes the tenfold Dharma, the twenty-two hardships and the twelve reflections —

*Yah sab bhav sattavan milkar, roke karmon ka aana.*
*Daat laga lo karma bali par, karmabandh se bach jana.*
all these fifty-seven states together stop the coming of karmas. Put the plug against mighty karma; escape from karmic bondage.

*Samyak bin na samvar hove, taate samyak dhar lena.*
*Uttam samyam palan karke, bhav sagar se tar lena.*
Without right faith there is no *samvara*; therefore take up right faith. Observing supreme restraint, cross over the ocean of existence.

## 9. Nirjara Bhavana — Shedding of Karma

*Jyon sarvar jal ruka sookhta, tapan padat hi bahu bhari.*
*Samvar roke aati vidhi ko, tap satta sokhan hari.*
As the water of a lake, its inflow stopped, dries up when the fierce heat falls on it — *samvara* stops the incoming karma, and austerity dries up the karma already in existence.

*Dvividh hai yah karma nirjara, aam daal par pak jaye.*
*Dooji paal pakave mali, yahi kaam tere aave.*
This shedding of karma is of two kinds: (one) the mango ripens on the branch; (the other) the gardener ripens it by artificial means — this second alone is of use to you.

*Pahli sabke satat ho rahi, dooji tap se hoti hai.*
*Udyam kar jo kare nirjara, vahi karma mal khoti hai.*
The first is happening continually for all; the second comes through austerity. He who sheds karma by effort — he loses the dirt of karma.

*Samvar sahit karo tap praani, mil jave mukti rani.*
*Karma kaat kar Shivpur jao, jaanat hai yah sab gyani.*
Perform austerity together with *samvara*, O creature; the Queen of Liberation will be won. Cutting away karma, go to the City of Liberation — all the wise know this.

## 10. Loka Bhavana — The Universe

*Teen shatak tetalees raju, purushakar hai lok kaha.*
*Anya dravya na jismein hote, vah to hai alok raha.*
Three hundred and forty-three cubic *rajus*, in the shape of a standing man — such is the universe (*loka*) said to be. Where no other substance exists — that is the non-universe (*aloka*).

*Kati par haath dhare nar koi, pag phaila kar khada rahe.*
*Vahi lok hai amit anadi, anant kaal tak pada rahe.*
Like a man standing with hands on his hips and feet apart — that is the universe, unmade, beginningless, remaining for endless time.

*Paap-punya se jeev jagat mein, nit sukh dukh ko bharta hai.*
*Apni karni aap kare phir, shir auron ke dharta hai.*
Through sin and merit the soul in the world ever fills itself with joy and sorrow; it does its own deeds, and then lays the blame on others' heads.

*Mithya moh hatao chetan, sab jag ki aasha chhodo.*
*Nij pad mein phir thir ho jao, Shivpur se nata jodo.*
Remove false delusion, O conscious one; give up all hope in the world. Become steady again in your own state; form your bond with the City of Liberation.

## 11. Bodhi-durlabha Bhavana — The Rarity of Enlightenment

*Durlabh nigod se bahar aana, durlabh tras bhav ka pana.*
*Anant bhavon mein bhatke phir bhi, durlabh nar bhav mil jana.*
Rare it is to come out of *nigoda*; rare to obtain birth as a mobile being. Even after wandering through infinite births, rare it is to obtain human birth.

*Desh kaal aayu nirog bal, durlabh uttam kul pana.*
*Durlabh Guru ki dharma deshana, durlabh samyak ho jana.*
(A good) country, time, life-span, health and strength — rare is it to obtain a noble family; rare is the Dharma-discourse of a Guru; rare is the attainment of right faith.

*Durlabh anuvrat, durlabh samyam, durlabh mahavrat ka dharna.*
*Uttam charya palan karke, maran samadhi ka karna.*
Rare are the minor vows, rare is restraint, rare the taking of the great vows; (rare) to observe supreme conduct and to die in *samadhi*.

*Durlabh shreni aarohan kar, durlabh shukla dhyan hona.*
*Durlabh se durlabh hai chetan, aathon karmon ko khona.*
Rare it is to ascend the ladder (*shreni*); rare is pure (*shukla*) meditation. Rarest of the rare, O conscious one, is the destruction of all eight karmas.

## 12. Dharma Bhavana — The True Dharma

*Kai nitya kshanik, ekanti, nirapekshata baat karen.*
*Nij par ka vah ghat karat hai, kunay apeksha kathan karen.*
Some speak of the eternal, some of the momentary, one-sidedly and without regard (to the other aspect); he destroys both self and other who speaks from the standpoint of a false *naya*.

*Janvani aur manvani ko, kahne wale ghoom rahe.*
*Apne apne mat ko lekar, Jinavani ko bhool rahe.*
Those who preach the words of men and the words of the mind are roaming about; each taking his own opinion, they are forgetting the *Jinavani*.

*Purvacharyon ki vani ko, mithya kahte rahte hain.*
*Agam par na shraddha karte, mithya mat ko bharte hain.*
They keep calling the words of the ancient Acharyas false; they place no faith in the Agam and fill themselves with false doctrine.

*Daya, Ratnatraya, Dharma, Ahimsa, das dharmon ko dhar lena.*
*"Prabhav" dharma bhavana karke, bhav sagar se tar lena.*
Take up compassion, the Three Jewels, Dharma, non-violence and the ten Dharmas. "Prabhav" — by reflecting on Dharma, cross over the ocean of existence.

## Conclusion

*Ab tak jo bhi gaye moksha mein, barah bhavan bhai hain.*
*Nij par mein hai bhed karan ki, yah hi sabki mai hai.*
Whoever has gone to *moksha* until now has contemplated the twelve reflections. Making the distinction between self and other — this is the mother of them all.

*Tirthankar se mahapurush ne, bhi hai iska manan kiya.*
*Vitaragata upja karke, Muktipuri ko gaman kiya.*
Even great souls like the Tirthankars have contemplated these; generating freedom from passion, they went to the City of Liberation.

*Mohbhav ko chhod sayane, barah bhavan ko bhavo.*
*Chidanand chidroop swaroopi, Ratnatraya ko pa jao.*
Give up the state of delusion, O wise one; contemplate the twelve reflections. O you whose nature is bliss of consciousness, the very form of consciousness — attain the Three Jewels.

*Syadvad aru anekant-may, Vitarag ki vani hai.*
*"Prabhav" aatma-sadhana karke, Shiv-ramani ko pani hai.*
Full of *Syadvada* and *Anekanta* is the word of the Passionless One. "Prabhav" — by practising the discipline of the Self, the Bride of Liberation is to be won.
