# Charitra Chakravarti Shri 108 Shantisagar Question-Answer Quiz

*Shri Shantivirashivadharmajita "Vardhamana" Suribhyo Namah*
*(Salutations to the Acharyas Shanti, Vira, Shiva, Dharma, Ajita and "Vardhamana")*

## Childhood and Household Life

**Question 1 —** What was the birth-name of Acharya Shri Shantisagar Maharaj?
Answer — Satgauda Patil.

**Question 2 —** What was the name of Acharya Shri's father?
Answer — Bhimgauda Patil.

**Question 3 —** What was the name of Satgauda's mother?
Answer — Satyavati.

**Question 4 —** Where was Satgauda born?
Answer — At his maternal uncle's house, in Yelgul village, Maharashtra.

**Question 5 —** Which is Satgauda's ancestral village?
Answer — Bhojgram (Bhoj village).

**Question 6 —** When, and in which year, was Satgauda born?
Answer — On Ashadha Krishna 6, Vikram Samvat 1929, 1872 CE, on a Wednesday night.

**Question 7 —** In which lineage (*vansha*) and caste was Satgauda born?
Answer — In the Kshatriya lineage and the Chaturtha caste.

**Question 8 —** How many brothers did Satgauda have? Name them.
Answer — There were three brothers: Devgauda, Adigauda and Kumgauda.

**Question 9 —** How many sisters did Satgauda have? Name them.
Answer — There was one sister: Krishnabai.

**Question 10 —** When, and to whom, was Satgauda married?
Answer — At the age of 9, to a girl named Saraswati, aged 6.

**Question 11 —** When did Satgauda lose his wife?
Answer — Six months after the marriage.

**Question 12 —** What did the astrologer say on seeing Satgauda's horoscope?
Answer — "This child will be extremely religious. He will attain renown throughout the world, and will not get entangled in the affairs of the world."

**Question 13 —** How great was Satgauda's physical strength?
Answer — He would easily lift a sack of four *maunds* of rice; he would draw water from the well using the *mot* (large leather bucket); with both feet together he would leap across a stretch twelve *hasta* (cubits) long; and he could knock down six men at once.

**Question 14 —** What longing (*dohala*) arose in his mother while Satgauda was in her womb?
Answer — "May I worship Jinendra Bhagwan with one hundred and eight thousand-petalled lotuses."

**Question 15 —** From where were the lotus flowers procured?
Answer — From the lake of the King of Kolhapur.

**Question 16 —** Which rivers had their confluence near Bhojgram?
Answer — The Vedganga and the Dudhganga rivers.

**Question 17 —** Whom did Satgauda carry across the confluence of the rivers?
Answer — The Munirajs who came to Bhojgram.

**Question 18 —** How did Satgauda carry the Munirajs across the river?
Answer — He would seat them on his shoulders and carry them across.

**Question 19 —** What was Satgauda's life like in childhood?
Answer — He remained calm in disposition, never quarrelled with anyone, did not play dirty games, and stayed away from worldly amusements. From the very beginning his inclination was toward dispassion (*vitaraga*).

**Question 20 —** How did Satgauda conduct himself toward society?
Answer — He treated everyone — poor and rich alike — with equality.

**Question 21 —** What did Satgauda tell society regarding animals?
Answer — "Never underfeed the animals; keep giving them food and water on time; do not tie them with a short rope; do not beat them with whips," and so on.

**Question 22 —** What was the name of Satgauda's grandfather, and for how many generations had the family held the right of Patil?
Answer — His grandfather's name was Girigauda, and the family had held the right of Patil for seven generations.

**Question 23 —** Who is a Patil, and what authority does he have?
Answer — The Patil is the protector (headman) of the village, and he has the authority to punish an offender for up to one month.

**Question 24 —** How did Satgauda conduct his business?
Answer — He sat at a cloth shop, and did business with a detached attitude. "When one is going to leave the house and go out anyway, what is the use of absorption and attachment in this?" He conducted the work with an attitude of indifference.

**Question 25 —** What effect did Satgauda's instruction have on Rudrappa, a devotee of Vedanta?
Answer — He began to drink filtered water and stopped eating at night. Once, while Rudrappa was eating at night, Satgauda had shown him directly the moths and other creatures falling into his food.

**Question 26 —** What was his non-attachment in business like?
Answer — When he sat at the shop and someone came to buy cloth, he would say: "If you want cloth, choose it to your own liking, measure and cut it with your own hands, and write it in the account book with your own hand."

**Question 27 —** From where did Satgauda's ancestors come to Bhojgram, and why?
Answer — There was the terrible oppression of Tipu Sultan in Bijapur district; therefore they loaded all their property onto carts drawn by eight bullocks and came from Saraddi village.

**Question 28 —** How did Satgauda show compassion toward birds?
Answer — When birds came to eat grain in his field, far from driving them away, he made arrangements for water for them to drink as well. When the harvest ripened, the grain in his field was more than in the fields of others.

**Question 29 —** Among the Munis whom Satgauda carried across the river, who was the chief Muniraj?
Answer — Muni 108 Shri Adisagar Ji of Borgaon.

**Question 30 —** What did Satgauda say to the Muniraj while carrying him across the river?
Answer — "I am taking you across the river; you take me across the ocean of worldly existence (*bhava-sagara*)."

**Question 31 —** When and where did Satgauda take the vow of *ekashana* (eating once a day)?
Answer — At Sammed Shikhar Ji, at the age of 32.

**Question 32 —** Where did Satgauda renounce ghee and oil?
Answer — At Sammed Shikhar Ji.

**Question 33 —** Whom did Satgauda carry on his shoulder to perform the *vandana* of Sammed Shikhar Ji, and what inspiration did he receive from this?
Answer — He carried an old mother on his back and had her perform the *vandana*; from this he received a powerful inspiration to become a renunciant (*tyagi*).

**Question 34 —** Whom did Satgauda help perform the *vandana* of Rajgrihi?
Answer — He carried an old man on his back and had him perform the *vandana*.

**Question 35 —** For how many years did Satgauda, while a householder, take *ekantara ahara* (food on alternate days), and what did he take as food?
Answer — For five years; and he took milk and rice.

**Question 36 —** How did Satgauda save the life of a frog?
Answer — A snake was about to eat a frog; Satgauda flung the *lota* (water pot) in his hand forcefully onto a rock and saved it.

**Question 37 —** When did Satgauda first come into contact with a snake?
Answer — While he was lying in the field, a snake put its head out of the ground, touched his neck again and again, and then went back into its hole.

**Question 38 —** Why was Satgauda not afraid even at the touch of the snake?
Answer — Great souls are fearless from childhood.

**Question 39 —** When his parents told Satgauda to marry, what would he say?
Answer — "If you put too much pressure on me, I shall become a Muni."

**Question 40 —** At what age did Satgauda take the vow of lifelong *brahmacharya* (celibacy)?
Answer — At the age of sixteen.

**Question 41 —** What did the village people call Satgauda?
Answer — Everyone called him "Appa" (grandfather/elder).

**Question 42 —** How was Satgauda at horse-riding and marksmanship?
Answer — He was of the first rank in horsemanship. As for marksmanship — he had never taken a gun in his hand in his life; yet once, when marksmanship was being discussed in jest, he took aim with a gun with full concentration and pierced a coconut. After that he never touched a gun again.

**Question 43 —** Whom did Satgauda save from the noose of hanging, and how?
Answer — Fifteen to twenty men used to come to Satgauda at night to hear the scriptures; among them Malgauda Patil also used to come. One day he did not come. Satgauda went looking for him to his field; there Malgauda had put the noose around his neck, but was still alive. Satgauda saved Malgauda from dying.

**Question 44 —** What was Satgauda's devotion to Munis like, and what did he say to his mother?
Answer — When *nirgrantha* Munirajs came to the settlement he would serve them greatly. He would say to his mother: "I too will become a Muni; I shall not stay here."

**Question 45 —** From when did Satgauda's feelings of dispassion (*vairagya*) begin?
Answer — From the age of 17–18 he had the feelings for *nirgrantha diksha*.

**Question 46 —** Then what was it that prevented Satgauda from becoming a Muni sooner?
Answer — Satgauda's father insisted: "As long as we are alive, stay at home and practise Dharma." To obey his father's command he had to remain at home.

**Question 47 —** The Muni Maharaj whom Satgauda carried across the river — Adisagar Maharaj — after how many days used he to take food?
Answer — He took food after seven days; on the remaining days he fasted.

**Question 48 —** What did Muni Adisagar Maharaj take as food?
Answer — He took only one item in his food. When he took sugarcane juice, he took no other substance apart from it.

**Question 49 —** When Muni Adisagar Ji came to Bhojgram, where did he stay, and where did he live on other days?
Answer — When he came to Bhoj he stayed only in Satgauda's shop and slept there that day; on other days he lived in the forest.

**Question 50 —** What was the cause of Satgauda's dispassion?
Answer — The impressions (*samskara*) of previous births.

**Question 51 —** From whom did Satgauda take *Kshullaka diksha*?
Answer — From Muni Shri Devendrakirti Maharaj.

**Question 52 —** Where did Satgauda take *Kshullaka diksha*?
Answer — In Uttur village.

**Question 53 —** When did he take *Kshullaka diksha*?
Answer — On Jyeshtha Shukla 13 (Trayodashi), Shaka Samvat 1837, 1916 CE, V.S. 1972.

**Question 54 —** Where was his first chaturmas as a Kshullaka?
Answer — In Kognoli village.

**Question 55 —** What was Satgauda's name after becoming a Kshullaka?
Answer — Kshullaka Shanti Sagar.
